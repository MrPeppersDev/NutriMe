>> title: Hormone Balancing Creamy Nettle Soup
>> servings: 4
>> source: http://www.theawesomegreen.com/hormone-balancing-creamy-nettle-soup/
>> attribution: theawesomegreen.com

-- Ingredients

@fresh stinging nettles (rinsed and drained){1%cup}
@parsnip (peeled and finely chopped){1}
@carrot (peeled and finely chopped){1}
@celery stalks (finely chopped){2}
@onion (finely diced){1}
@garlic cloves (crushed){2}
@potato (peeled and cut into small cubes){1}
@parsley root (peeled and finely chopped){1}
@tahini{2%tsp}
@purified water{4%cups}
@sea salt{1/4%tsp}
@Freshly ground black pepper{}
@freshly squeezed lemon juice{1%tbsp}
@Black sesame (to garnish){}

-- Instructions

Add the vegetables into a soup pot, cover them with water and bring to boil over medium heat.

Simmer for 30 minutes, then remove from the heat.

Scald the nettles into the soup pot for two or three minutes, then transfer everything into the blender.

Add the tahini, lemon, salt and freshly ground black pepper, and process to obtain a creamy soup.

Serve warm, garnished with black sesame seeds.
