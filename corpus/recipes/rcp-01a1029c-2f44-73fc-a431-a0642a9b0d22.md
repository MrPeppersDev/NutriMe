-- Ingredients

@fresh spinach, washed and drained{4 1/2%cups}
@sliced mushrooms, drained (2 ounce cans){1%can}
@water chestnuts, diced (5 oz can){1%cup}
@grapefruits, sectioned and diced{2}
@vegetable oil{1/4%cup}
@vinegar{1%tablespoon}
@us fluid ounces grapefruit juice{2}
@soy sauce, low-sodium{1%tablespoon}
@hot pepper sauce{1/4%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@dry mustard{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Tear spinach coarsely and place in large salad bowl.

Add mushrooms, water chestnuts, and grapefruit.

Mix together oil, vinegar, grapefruit juice, soy sauce, hot pepper sauce, salt, and dry mustard.

Toss dressing with spinach mixture and serve.
