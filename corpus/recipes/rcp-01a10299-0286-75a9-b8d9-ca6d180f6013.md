-- Ingredients

@handfuls fresh baby arugula{2}
@freshly-grated Parmesan (plus extra shavings as garnish){2%Tablespoons}
@good-quality olive oil{2%teaspoons}
@freshly-squeezed lemon juice{2%teaspoons}
@freshly-cracked black pepper{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Add arugula and Parmesan to a large mixing bowl. Drizzle evenly with olive oil and lemon juice, and sprinkle with black pepper. Toss until combined.

Serve immediately, garnished with extra Parmesan if desired.
