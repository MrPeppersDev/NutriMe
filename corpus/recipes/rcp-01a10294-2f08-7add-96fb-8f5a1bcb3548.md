-- Ingredients

@almonds{1%cup}
@raisins{1%cup}
@cinnamon{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Rinse the raisins and almonds with water.

Toss them in the food processor with the cinnamon. When sufficiently mixed, form into balls or bar shapes.
