>> title: Hibiscus, lemongrass and Basil Honey Sweet Iced Tea.
>> servings: 1
>> source: http://www.halfbakedharvest.com/hibiscus-lemongrass-basil-and-honey-sweet-iced-tea/
>> attribution: halfbakedharvest.com

-- Ingredients

@water{5%cups}
@lemongrass stalk (thinly sliced, plus more stalks for serving*){1}
@honey (or more to taste (may sub coconut sugar or maple syrup for vegan version)){1/4%cup}
@dried hibiscus flowers{1/2%cup}
@loosely packed fresh basil leaves (plus more for serving){3/4%cup}
@fresh squeezed lime juice (plus lime slices for serving){1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Bring the water to a boil in a medium size pot. Remove from the heat and add the lemongrass, honey, hibiscus flowers and basil leaves. Cover and let steep for 20 minutes. Strain into a pitcher and discard the used lemongrass, hibiscus flowers and basil.

Stir in the lime juice and taste, if needed add more honey. Chill until cold. Serve over ice with a fresh lemongrass stalk, basil and lime. Enjoy!
