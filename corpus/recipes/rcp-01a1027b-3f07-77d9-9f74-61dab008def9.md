-- Ingredients

@frozen wild blueberries{1%cup}
@frozen strawberries{1%cup}
@baby spinach{2%cups}
@maple syrup{2%tablespoons}
@water{1 1/4%cups}
@almond butter{1-2%tablespoons}
@hulled hemp seeds{3%tablespoons}
@inch fresh ginger, peeled and roughly chopped{1}
@rolled oats{1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Place all ingredients into the blender and blend until super smooth.

Enjoy!
