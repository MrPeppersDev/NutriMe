-- Ingredients

@sugar{2%cups}
@cocoa powder (unsweetened){3%tablespoons}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@margarine{1/2%cup}
@peanut butter{1%cup}
@water{1/2%cup}
@rolled oats{4%cups}
@non-fat dry milk{1%cup}
@raisins{1%cup}
@vanilla{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In large sauce pan, bring the first six ingredients to a full, rolling boil for 1 minute, stirring as needed.

Remove from heat. Add oats, dry milk, and raisins and vanilla to boiled mixture.

Mix well.

Drop by tablespoons onto waxed paper and let stand until cool, about 15 to 20 minutes.
