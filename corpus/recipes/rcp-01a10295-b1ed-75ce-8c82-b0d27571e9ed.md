-- Ingredients

@shredded cheddar cheese (or any other firmer cheese like Parmesan){}
@desired herbs & spices (optional){}

-- Instructions

Preheat oven to 350 degrees.

Mix any herbs and spices you desire with shredded cheese. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper – do NOT skip this step!

Sprinkle about a tablespoon of shredded cheese in a flat circle, leaving about two inches in between for them to spread out.

Bake in the middle rack of the oven for about 5-10 minutes until they turn golden brown. Let cool before serving
