-- Ingredients

@white flour{8%cups}
@sugar{1 1/2%cups}
@baking soda{1%tablespoon}
@ginger (dried, ground){3%tablespoons}
@cinnamon (dried, ground){3%tablespoons}
@cloves (dried, ground){1%teaspoon}
@salt{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a large bowl. Mix well.

Store mix in a covered jar until ready to use.
