-- Ingredients

@banana (fresh or frozen){1}
@small pineapple - peeled, cored, and chopped{1}
@turmeric powder{1 1/2%teaspoon}
@fresh ginger (according to preference){1%piece}
@almond milk (or any other milk alternative){3/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Place the smoothie ingredients into a blender.

Blend until smooth and creamy.

Pour into serving glasses and enjoy!
