-- Ingredients

@(24 ounces) spaghetti sauce, meatless, low-sodium{1%jar}
@water{1/3%cup}
@cottage cheese, fat-free{6%ounces}
@egg, slightly beaten{1}
@oregano{1/2%teaspoon}
@dry macaroni{1%cup}
@mozzarella cheese, part skim, shredded{4%ounces}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a bowl combine sauce and water.

In another bowl combine cottage cheese, egg and oregano.

Grease 1 quart casserole.

Spoon in 1/3 sauce.

Layer with 1/2 cup macaroni, 1/2 cottage cheese mixture, 1/2 mozzarella cheese and 1/3 of the spaghetti sauce.

Repeat layers one more time.

Cover with aluminum foil.

Bake in a 350 °F oven for 45 minutes.

Uncover and bake 15 minutes longer.
