-- Ingredients

@cooked cooled chicken thighs (about 3 cups shredded){4}
@vegetable oil{2%tablespoons}
@low-fat yogurt{1/8%cup}
@curry powder (or more to taste){1%tablespoon}
@celery stalks (diced or sliced){2}
@apple (cored and diced){1}
@mandarin oranges, canned, drained{1/4%cup}
@chopped fresh cilantro (and/or basil leaves){1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Put chicken in a bowl and add remaining ingredients. Mix to combine.

Cover and refrigerate at least one hour and up three days.
