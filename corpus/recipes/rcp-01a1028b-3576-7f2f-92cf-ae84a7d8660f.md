-- Ingredients

@lettuce mix{12%ounces}
@ripe bananas{2}
@medium apples, sliced (14 oz. of sliced apples){2 1/2}
@unsalted dry roasted peanuts{3/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Whisk together dressing ingredients.

Slice bananas.

In a large salad bowl, toss mixed greens, banana and apple slices, peanuts, and dressing.
