-- Ingredients

@black beans{1/3%cup}
@tomato sliced and diced (~2 Tbsp tomatoes, diced){1}
@onion, diced{1%tablespoon}
@lemon juice{1%teaspoon}
@ripe, fresh avocado, halved, pitted and peeled{1/2}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a bowl, combine black beans, tomatoes and onion. Squeeze in fresh lemon juice.

Serve in fresh avocado half.
