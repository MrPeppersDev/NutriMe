-- Ingredients

@medium cucumbers (about 1 1/2 lb. total), thinly sliced{3}
@red onion, thinly sliced{1/4}
@plain Greek yogurt{1/3%c}
@fresh lemon juice{1 1/2%tbsp}
@finely chopped fresh dill, plus more for serving{1%tbsp}
@Kosher salt{}
@Freshly ground black pepper{}

-- Instructions

In a large bowl, toss cucumbers and onion.

In a medium bowl, whisk yogurt, lemon juice, and dill until smooth; season with salt and pepper.

Pour dressing over cucumbers and onions and toss to coat. Top with more dill.
