-- Ingredients

@black beans, low-sodium (15 ounce, drained and rinsed){1%can}
@onion powder{1/4%teaspoon}
@dried oregano{1/4%teaspoon}
@garlic powder{1/8%teaspoon}
@cayenne pepper{1/8%teaspoon}
@salsa (thick and chunky){1/2%cup}
@sour cream (light){1/2%cup}
@green onion (chopped){1/4%cup}
@cheddar cheese, reduced fat (grated){2%ounces}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mash beans and mix with onion powder, dried oregano, garlic powder, and cayenne pepper. Spread on a serving dish.

Top with salsa, sour cream, green onions, and grated cheese.
