-- Ingredients

@ripe, fresh avocado, halved, pitted, peeled and diced{1}
@whole grain pita bread pockets, cut in half{2}
@canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained{1/4%cup}
@crumbled Cotija or Feta cheese{2%tablespoons}
@salt and pepper, to taste{}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Fill pita pocket half with avocado, chickpeas and cheese, dividing evenly. Heat in microwave for 15 to 20 seconds if desired. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
