-- Ingredients

@can of sweetened condensed milk{14%oz}
@heavy whipping creme{1%pint}
@to 3 drops of peppermint extract{2}
@crushed peppermint candies{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Instructions

Whip the heavy whipping creme in a freezer safe glass or metal container (one with a lid) using an electric hand mixer until firm

Add in the sweetened condensed milk and stir well.

Add in peppermint extract and crushed candies and stir well

Place lid on container and allow to freeze 10 to 12 hours

Scoop into bowls and enjoy
