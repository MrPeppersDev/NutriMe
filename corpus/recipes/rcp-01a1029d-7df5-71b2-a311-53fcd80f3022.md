-- Ingredients

@cottage cheese, low-fat{1%cup}
@yogurt, low-fat plain (1/4 cup){4%tablespoons}
@onion (chopped, or chives){1%tablespoon}
@parsley (dried){1%teaspoon}
@dill (dried){1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place all the ingredients in a blender.

Blend all the ingredients thoroughly.

Pour the mixture into a clean container.

Cover and chill.
