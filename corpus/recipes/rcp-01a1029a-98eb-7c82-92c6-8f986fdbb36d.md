-- Ingredients

@English muffin (whole wheat){1}
@whipped fat-free strawberry cream cheese{2%tablespoons}
@strawberries, sliced{2}
@grapes, sliced{2}
@mandarin oranges{2%slices}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toast the English muffin until golden brown. Spread cream cheese on toasted muffin.

Arrange sliced strawberries, grapes, and orange slices on top of the cream cheese.
