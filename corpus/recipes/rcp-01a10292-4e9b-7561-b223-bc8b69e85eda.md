-- Ingredients

@sweet cherries, pitted and cut in half{1/2%cup}
@cubed cantaloupe{1%cup}
@seedless green grapes, cut in half{1%cup}
@medium banana, peeled and sliced{1}
@orange juice (juice from 1/2 orange){1/4%cup}
@flaked coconut (optional){1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a large bowl, mix together the cut fruit.

Pour orange juice over fruit and stir in coconut, if desired.
