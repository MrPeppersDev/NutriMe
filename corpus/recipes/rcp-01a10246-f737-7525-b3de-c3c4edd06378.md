-- Ingredients

@orange juice{1%cup}
@frozen banana{1}
@fresh or ground ginger{1%teaspoon}
@Wedderspoon manuka honey{1%tablespoon}
@cinnamon{1/2%teaspoon}
@turmeric powder{1%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@shredded carrots{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Add all of the ingredients to a high-speed blender, and blend until the smoothie is nice and creamy, about 30-45 seconds.

Enjoy immediately!
