-- Ingredients

@vanilla almond milk{1%cup}
@vanilla Greek yogurt{1/2%cup}
@old fashioned oats{1/4%cup}
@brown sugar{1%Tablespoon}
@cinnamon (or 2-3 drops of Cinnamon Bark Essential Oil){1/4%teaspoon}
@frozen banana (or fresh banana, but add in 3-4 ice cubes with fresh){1}

-- Instructions

Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
