-- Ingredients

@Servings of Better Baking Mix (2 cups){2}
@vegetable oil{1/4%cup}
@water{1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 400 °F. Lightly grease baking sheet.

Add oil and water to the dry mix. Stir just until all ingredients are wet.

Drop spoonsful 4 inches apart on baking sheet.

Bake 10 to 12 minutes until lightly browned.
