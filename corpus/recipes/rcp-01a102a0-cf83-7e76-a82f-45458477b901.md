-- Ingredients

@celery (stalks, medium - washed & sliced){3}
@onion (large, chopped){1}
@tomato puree (low sodium){1/2%cup}
@potatoes (sliced){1 1/2%cups}
@carrot (washed and sliced){1 1/2%cups}
@clams (6 1/2 ounces each, chopped, drained){2%cans}
@thyme (dried){1 1/2%teaspoons}
@bay leaf{1}
@dashes black peppercorns{2}
@water{1 1/2%cups}
@tomato juice (low sodium ){2%cups}
@parsley flakes (dried){1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Scrub potatoes well to remove any eyes or blemishes.

In a slow cooker, combine all ingredients; stir.

Cover and cook on low for 8 to 10 hours or until the vegetables are tender.

Remove bay leaf and peppercorns before serving.
