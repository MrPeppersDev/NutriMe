-- Ingredients

@gin (such as Hendrick's) ((1 1/2 ounces) ){3%tablespoons}
@fresh, strained lime juice ((1/2 ounce) ){1%tablespoon}
@simple syrup ((1/4 ounce) ){1 1/2%teaspoons}
@Green Chartreuse ((1/6 ounce) ){1%teaspoon}
@large mint leaves, plus a sprig for garnish (optional){4}
@ice cubes{}

-- Instructions

In a cocktail shaker combine the gin, lime juice, simple syrup, chartreuse, and mint leaves.

Add enough ice cubes to fill the shaker halfway.

Shake vigorously for 30 seconds, then strain into a coupe.

Garnish with a mint sprig and serve.
