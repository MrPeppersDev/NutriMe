-- Ingredients

@cucumber with peel, chopped{1%cup}
@low-sodium canned corn{1%cup}
@cherry tomatoes, chopped{1%cup}
@red pepper, chopped (2 Tablespoons){1/8%cup}
@green pepper, chopped (2 Tablespoons){1/8%cup}
@red wine vinegar{2%tablespoons}
@garlic powder{1/2%teaspoon}
@ground cumin{1/2%teaspoon}
@dried cilantro or coriander{1/4%teaspoon}
@ground black pepper{1/8%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine ingredients and mix well.

Serve cold.
