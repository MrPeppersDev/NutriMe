-- Ingredients

@olive oil{1/4%C}
@garlic, minced, or 1/8 tsp garlic powder{1%clove}
@coarsely chopped onion{1 1/3%C}
@coarsely chopped celery and leaves{1 1/2%C}
@(6 oz) no-salt-added tomato paste{1%can}
@chopped fresh parsley{1%Tbsp}
@sliced carrots, fresh or frozen{1%C}
@shredded cabbage{4 3/4%C}
@(1 lb) no-salt-added tomatoes, cut up{1%can}
@(15 1/2 oz) low-sodium red kidney beans, drained and rinsed{1%can}
@frozen peas{1 1/2%C}
@fresh green beans{1 1/2%C}
@Dash hot sauce{}
@water{11%C}
@spaghetti, finely broken, uncooked{2%C}

-- Instructions

Heat oil in a 4-quart saucepan.

Add garlic, onion, and celery and sauté about 5 minutes.

Add all remaining ingredients except spaghetti, and stir until ingredients are well mixed.

Bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer about 45 minutes or until vegetables are tender.

Add uncooked spaghetti and simmer 2-3 minutes only. Serve warm.
