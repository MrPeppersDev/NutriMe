-- Ingredients

@milk{1%cup}
@rose hibiscus concentrate{3%tablespoons}
@edible dried rose petals{1%teaspoon}
@Ice

-- Instructions

In a cocktail shaker or a wide mouth water bottle with a tight lid, shake to combine milk and rose hibiscus concentrate.

Pour rose latte into a cup of ice and top with dried rose petals.
