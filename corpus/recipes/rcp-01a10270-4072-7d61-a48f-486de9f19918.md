-- Ingredients

@Trader Joe's Frozen pizza Crust{1}
@- 1/2 cup Trader Joe's Limone Alfredo Sauce (spread sauce evenly over pizza crust ){1/4}
@Arugula{2%cups}
@Proscuitto{3%slices}
@Mozzarella Cheese{1%cup}
@olive oil (lightly drizzle olive oil over the top of the cheese){}

-- Instructions

Preheat oven to 425 degrees

Remove frozen pizza crust from package

Spread Limone Alfredo sauce over pizza crust. Make sure to spread a small amount - spread evenly over center toward the edges of the pizza crust

Next add arugula - spread evenly.

Break apart prosciuitto into small pieces and add throughout the pizza.

Spread mozzarella cheese evenly over the pizza.

Drizzle with a very small amount of olive oil.

Bake at 425 for about 10 min. depending on how cooked you like your cheese and crust. Keep and eye on it - this particular crust bakes fast!

Allow pizza to cool for 5 min. before cutting.
