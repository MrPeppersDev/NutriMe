-- Ingredients

@milk, nonfat powdered{2%cups}
@cornstarch{3/4%cup}
@chicken bouillon, low-sodium{12%teaspoons}
@onion flakes, dried{2%tablespoons}
@Italian seasoning{2%teaspoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a plastic bag.

Mix well.
