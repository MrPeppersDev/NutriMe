-- Ingredients

@(10 oz) frozen, chopped broccoli (or fresh){1%package}
@(10 oz) frozen mixed vegetables, thawed{1%package}
@(10.75 oz) cream of mushroom soup, low sodium{1%can}
@us fluid ounces 1% low fat milk{10 3/4}
@grated cheddar cheese, low fat (can use 1/4 to 1/2 cup){1/3%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In saucepan, prepare broccoli according to directions. Drain off water.

Add mixed vegetables, cream of mushroom soup, and one soup can full of milk to the broccoli. Stir over low heat until steaming hot and vegetables are cooked. Add cheese, stirring until melted.

Serve hot.

Refrigerate leftovers.
