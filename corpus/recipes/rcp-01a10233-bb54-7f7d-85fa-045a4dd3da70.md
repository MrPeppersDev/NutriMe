>> title: 20-Minute Chicken Creole
>> servings: 4

>> attribution: NHLBI Delicious Heart Healthy Eating

-- Ingredients

@boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into thin strips{12%oz}
@canned whole peeled tomatoes, chopped{1%C}
@chili sauce (look for lowest sodium version){1%C}
@green bell pepper, rinsed and chopped{1 1/2%C}
@celery, rinsed and chopped{1 1/2%C}
@onion, chopped{1/4%C}
@garlic, minced (about 2–3 cloves){1%Tbsp}
@fresh basil, rinsed, dried, and chopped (or 1 tsp dried){1%Tbsp}
@fresh parsley, rinsed, dried, and chopped (or 1 tsp dried){1%Tbsp}
@crushed red pepper{1/4%tsp}
@salt{1/4%tsp}
@Cooking spray{}

-- Instructions

Spray sauté pan with cooking spray. Preheat over high heat.

Cook chicken in hot sauté pan, stirring for 3–5 minutes. Reduce heat.

Add tomatoes with juice, chili sauce, green pepper, celery, onion, garlic, basil, parsley, crushed red pepper, and salt. Bring to boil over high heat, and then reduce heat to simmer.

Simmer, covered, for 10 minutes.
