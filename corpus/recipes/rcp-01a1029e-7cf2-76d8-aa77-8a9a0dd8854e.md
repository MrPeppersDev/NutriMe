-- Ingredients

@garbanzo beans, cooked (also known as chickpeas){2%cups}
@garlic (minced){2%cloves}
@lemon juice{1/4%cup}
@sesame tahini [sesame paste] (or substitute peanut butter for a sweet taste){1%tablespoon}
@olive oil{2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mash the garbanzo beans until smooth (if you have a blender, put the beans and lemon juice into it and blend).

Add the garlic, lemon juice, tahini and oil. Mix well.
