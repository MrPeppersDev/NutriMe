-- Ingredients

@oatmeal, uncooked{3%cups}
@coconut, shredded or flaked{1/2%cup}
@pecans, chopped (or try walnuts or peanuts){1%cup}
@honey{1/4%cup}
@margarine, melted{1/4%cup}
@cinnamon{1 1/2%teaspoons}
@raisins{2/3%cup}

-- Instructions

Heat oven to 350 ºF. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl, except raisins, mix well.

Bake in 13x9 inch baking pan at 350 ºF for 25 to 30 minutes or until golden brown. Stir every 5 minutes.

Stir in raisins. Cool thoroughly. Store in tightly covered container.
