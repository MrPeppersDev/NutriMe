-- Ingredients

@medium carrots, peeled and grated{4}
@raisins{1/4%cup}
@sugar{2%teaspoons}
@lemon, juiced{1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a medium bowl, thoroughly mix carrots, raisins, sugar, and lemon juice.

Serve chilled.
