-- Ingredients

@yogurt (vanilla, fat-free){2%cups}
@pudding mix (instant, lemon, 3 Tablespoons){1%ounce}
@graham crackers (rectangles, crushed){8}
@mandarin orange (4 ounce, slices, drained or your favorite fruit){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine vanilla yogurt and pudding mix; gently stir together.

Layer bottom of individual dishes with crushed graham crackers. Reserve a few crumbs to sprinkle on top.

Pour pudding mixture over cracker crumbs.

Top with mandarin orange slices or your favorite fruit. Sprinkle with remaining crumbs.
