-- Ingredients

@yogurt, fruit-flavored (low-fat){1/2%cup}
@banana (medium){1/2}
@grapes, seedless{1/2%cup}
@dry crunchy cereal (Grape Nuts or granola cereals are good){1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Peel and slice the banana.

Spoon the grapes into a tall plastic cup.

Put 3 spoonsful of yogurt on top of the grapes.

Spoon sliced bananas on top of the yogurt.

Add the rest of the yogurt.

Sprinkle the cereal on top.
