-- Ingredients

@apples (large, peeled, cored, and sliced){4}
@cranberries (fresh or frozen){2 1/2%cups}
@sugar{3/4%cup}
@flour (all purpose){1/4%cup}
@apple pie spice{1%teaspoon}
@pie crust (prepared){1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Stir all ingredients (except for the pie crust) together in a medium sized mixing bowl and place in a 10-inch deep dish pie pan.

Place one pie crust on top of the fruits. Cut 3 or 4 slits to allow the steam to escape.

Bake at 375 °F for about an hour. Serve warm. Refrigerate any leftovers.
