-- Ingredients

@Vegetable Oil{2%cups}
@Achiote Seeds{4%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Heat the vegetable or olive oil and Achiote seeds in a small skillet over medium heat for about 2 to 3 minutes.
Don't let the seeds turn black. Remove the skillet from the heat and let stand for 5 minutes more. Strain the oil and store for up to 15 days at room temperature in a jar with a tight lid.
