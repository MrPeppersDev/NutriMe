-- Ingredients

@mango (peeled, pitted, and diced, or 1 cup thawed frozen chunks){1}
@onion, red (diced){1%tablespoon}
@cilantro (1 tablespoon, optional, fresh or dried){}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@lime juice (bottled or fresh){2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a bowl.

Serve with baked tortilla chips.
