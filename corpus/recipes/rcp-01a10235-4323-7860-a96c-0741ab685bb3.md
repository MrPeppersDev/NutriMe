-- Ingredients

@nonfat plain yogurt{1%C}
@medium strawberries{6}
@pineapple, crushed, canned in juice{1%C}
@medium banana{1}
@vanilla extract{1%tsp}
@ice cubes{4}

-- Instructions

Place all ingredients in blender, and puree until smooth.

Serve in frosted glasses.
