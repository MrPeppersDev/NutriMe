-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){1%tablespoon}
@eggs{6}
@fat-free milk{1/2%cup}
@low-fat cheese, grated{1/2%cup}
@garlic powder{1%teaspoon}
@oregano{1 1/2%teaspoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.

Put oil in a medium baking dish or small cake pan and heat in the oven for a few minutes.

In a medium bowl, beat eggs. Mix in remaining ingredients. Pour into hot pan.

Bake 20 minutes or until eggs are firm. Serve immediately.
