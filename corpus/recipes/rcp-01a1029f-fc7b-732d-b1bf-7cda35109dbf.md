-- Ingredients

@banana{1}
@non-fat strawberry yogurt{1/2%cup}
@whole-grain cereal{1/2%cup}
@seedless grapes, halved{1/4%cup}
@strawberries, sliced{1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Peel and cut the banana in half, lengthwise.

Place the banana on a plate.

Top banana with yogurt and sprinkle with cereal and fruit.
