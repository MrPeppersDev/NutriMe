>> notes: Bosnian cuisine contains lots of fresh produce. Tomatoes, peppers, carrots, and beans are common in everyday menus. To reduce cost, frozen and/or canned vegetables can be substituted for fresh vegetables. Learn more about: Cabbage Carrots Tomatoes Potatoes Onions Garlic Bell Peppers

-- Ingredients

@beef stew meat{2 1/4%pounds}
@cabbage{1%head}
@large carrots (2 or 3){2 1/2}
@large fresh tomatoes{3 1/2}
@large potatoes{3 1/2}
@medium onions{2}
@garlic cloves{2}
@green peppers{2}
@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of your choice){1/2%tablespoon}
@vinegar{1/2%tablespoon}
@salt and pepper (to taste, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Chop all the vegetables.

Combine with the meat and seasonings in a large pot.

Add enough water to completely cover the mixture and add the oil and vinegar.

Cook, covered, for two to three hours on low heat until everything is well done.
