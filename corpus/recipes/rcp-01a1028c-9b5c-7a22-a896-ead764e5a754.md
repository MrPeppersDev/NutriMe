-- Ingredients

@large, ripe avocado{1}
@honeydew melon chunks (about 1 slice){1%cup}
@lime, juiced (1 1/2 tsp lime juice){}
@8 ounce milk (fat free){1%cup}
@fat-free yogurt (plain){1%cup}
@100% apple juice or white grape juice{1/2%cup}
@honey{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Cut avocado in half, remove pit.

Scoop out flesh, place in blender.

Add remaining ingredients; blend well.

Serve cold. (Keeps well in refrigerator up to 24 hours. If made ahead, stir gently before pouring into glasses.)
