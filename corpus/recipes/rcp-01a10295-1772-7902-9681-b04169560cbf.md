-- Ingredients

@frozen corn, thawed{2%cups}
@canned diced tomatoes, low-sodium{1%cup}
@(4 ounces) green chiles{1/2%can}
@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){1/2%tablespoon}
@lime juice{1%tablespoon}
@green onion, sliced{1/3%cup}
@fresh cilantro, chopped{2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl.

Mix well.
