-- Ingredients

@cubed watermelon{3%cups}
@blackberries{1 1/2%cups}
@finely chopped mint leaves{2%tablespoons}
@Juice of one lime{}

-- Instructions

In a large bowl, combine watermelon, blackberries, and mint. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the fruit. Gently toss and serve.
