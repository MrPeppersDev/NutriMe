-- Ingredients

@85% lean ground beef{3/4%pound}
@water (can take up to 2 cups water){1 1/2%cups}
@egg noodles, uncooked (or any shaped pasta){2%cups}
@servings of Eating Smart Seasoning Mix (1/2 cup){2}
@salt (optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Brown ground beef in a large skillet, drain the fat.

Add water, egg noodles or pasta, and seasoning mix. Stir.

Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer covered for 15-20 minutes or until noodles are tender.

Taste, then add a small amount of salt, if needed.

Refrigerate leftovers.
