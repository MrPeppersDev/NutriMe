-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){2%teaspoons}
@onion, chopped{1/2%cup}
@carrots, chopped{1/2%cup}
@thyme, dried{1%teaspoon}
@garlic cloves, minced{2}
@water (or low-sodium chicken broth){2%cups}
@tomatoes, diced{3/4%cup}
@chicken, cooked, skinned and cubed{1%cup}
@brown rice, cooked (or white rice){1/2%cup}
@kale, chopped (about one large leaf){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Heat oil in a medium sauce pan. Add onion and carrots. Sauté until vegetables are tender, about 5 to 8 minutes.

Add thyme and garlic. Sauté for one more minute.

Add water or broth, tomatoes, chicken, cooked rice, and kale.

Simmer for 5 to 10 minutes.
