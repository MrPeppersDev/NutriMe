>> servings: 6
>> source: https://www.myplate.gov/recipes/fruit-juice-slush
>> attribution: USDA MyPlate Kitchen
>> description: Blending your favorite fruit juice concentrate with ice and water will give you a quick and refreshing drink on a warm day.
>> calories_per_serving: 25.34
>> original_contributor: Lifeskills in Food Education, Food Skills Cookbook University of Hawaii at Manoa Cooperative Extension Food Stamps Nutrition Education Program
>> notes: Use any flavor of juice concentrate. If slush is too thick, add more water.

-- Ingredients

@us fluid ounces 100% fruit juice concentrate{12}
@us fluid ounces water{12}
@ice{3%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a blender, place juice concentrate, water, and half of ice.

To prevent spillage, do not pass the top line of the blender.

Blend while gradually adding remaining ice. Serve.
