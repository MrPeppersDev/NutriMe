>> title: Blood Orange Elderflower Gin Cocktail
>> servings: 1
>> source: http://www.thelittleepicurean.com/2016/02/blood-orange-elderflower-gin-cocktail.html#comments
>> attribution: thelittleepicurean.com

-- Ingredients

@gin{1%oz}
@St. Germain ((elderflower liqueur)){3/4%oz}
@lime juice (fresh squeezed){1/2%oz}
@ginger ale (chilled){2 1/2%oz}
@blood orange juice (fresh squeezed){1%oz}

-- Instructions

In a serving glass, combine gin, St. Germain, lime juice, and ginger ale. Top glass with ice. Slowly pour in blood orange juice. Garnish with blood orange slices and fresh lemon thyme sprigs, if desired. Serve immediately.
