-- Ingredients

@shredded chicken, cooked{1/4%pound}
@celery stalk, chopped{1}
@medium apple, chopped{1}
@light mayonnaise{1/3%cup}
@lemon, juiced{1}
@(1.5 ounces) box raisins{1}
@whole wheat bread{8%slices}
@walnuts, chopped (optional){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients except for bread together in a bowl.

Create 4 sandwiches on whole wheat bread.
