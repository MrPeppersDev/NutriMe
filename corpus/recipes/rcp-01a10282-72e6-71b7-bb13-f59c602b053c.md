-- Ingredients

@scoops (90g) PlantFusion vanilla bean protein powder ((see Notes!)){3}
@(50g) old-fashioned rolled oats{1/2%cup}
@(9g) poppy seeds{1%tbsp}
@(7g) Truvia{1/2%tbsp}
@freshly grated lemon zest ((about one medium)){1%tsp}
@(90mL) freshly squeezed lemon juice ((about two medium)){6%tbsp}
@(90mL) water{6%tbsp}

-- Instructions

In a large bowl, combined the protein powder, oats, poppy seeds, Truvia, and lemon zest. Add in the lemon juice and water, stirring until fully incorporated. Shape the mixture into 28 small spheres, and place into a Tupperware container. Seal and store in the refrigerator until ready to eat.
