-- Ingredients

@(15 ounce) cream-style corn, low-sodium{1%can}
@broccoli, cooked{3 3/4%cups}
@egg, beaten{1}
@cracker crumbs, crushed{1/2%cup}
@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix corn, broccoli, egg, cracker crumbs, and oil together in greased 1 1/2 quart casserole.

Mix topping ingredients together in small bowl. Sprinkle over corn mixture.

Bake at 350 °F for 40 minutes.
