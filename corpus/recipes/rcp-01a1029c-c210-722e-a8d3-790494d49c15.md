-- Ingredients

@pineapple chunks{1%cup}
@peach (cubed){1}
@banana (sliced){1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place fruit chunks on a skewer to make kabobs.

Grill or broil on low heat until the fruit is hot and slightly golden.
