-- Ingredients

@broccoli (chopped){6%cups}
@raisins{1%cup}
@red onion (medium, peeled and diced){1}
@sugar{2%tablespoons}
@bacon slices (8 slices, cooked and crumbled, optional){}
@lemon juice{2%tablespoons}
@mayonnaise, low-fat{3/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl, and mix well.

Chill for 1 to 2 hours.

Serve.
