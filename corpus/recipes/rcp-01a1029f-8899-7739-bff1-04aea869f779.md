-- Ingredients

@spinach (1 pound, fresh){8%cups}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@lemon juice{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash the spinach. Trim off the stems.

Put the spinach, black pepper, and lemon juice in a pan.

Cook over medium heat. Let the spinach boil for about 3 minutes, until just tender.
