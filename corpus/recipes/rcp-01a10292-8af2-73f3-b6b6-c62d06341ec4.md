-- Ingredients

@flour, all purpose{2%tablespoons}
@water{2%tablespoons}
@chicken broth, low-sodium{1%cup}
@chicken, cooked and diced{1%cup}
@salt (optional, 1/4 teaspoon){}
@black pepper (1/16 of a teaspoon){1%dash}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix 2 tablespoons flour and water in a pan until smooth.

Slowly stir in broth.

Cook over medium heat until thickened.

Add chicken, salt (optional) and pepper.
