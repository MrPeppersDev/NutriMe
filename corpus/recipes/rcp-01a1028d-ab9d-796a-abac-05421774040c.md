-- Ingredients

@trout fillets, cut into six pieces (or other fish){2%pounds}
@lime juice (about 2-3 limes){3%tablespoons}
@medium tomato, chopped{1}
@medium onion, chopped{1/2}
@cilantro, chopped{3%tablespoons}
@olive oil (or cooking oil of choice){1/2%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@red pepper (1/4 teaspoon, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 °F.

Pat fish dry and place in baking dish.

In a separate dish, mix remaining ingredients together and pour over fish.

Bake for 15 to 20 minutes or until fork-tender.
