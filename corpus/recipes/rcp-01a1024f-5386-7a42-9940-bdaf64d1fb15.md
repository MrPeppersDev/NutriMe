-- Ingredients

@chia seeds{1/4%cup}
@full-fat coconut milk{1%cup}
@ceremonial-grade matcha powder{1%tsp}
@vanilla extract{1/2%tsp}
@maple syrup{2%tbsp}

-- Instructions

Whisk all the ingredients together in a bowl until well combined. Let sit for 2-3 minutes then mix again. The mixture should thicken slightly and become viscous.

Cover and place in the fridge for at least two hours before serving or if you have time, overnight.

Optionally top these chia puddings with some yogurt and a sprinkle of matcha powder
