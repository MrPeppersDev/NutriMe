-- Ingredients

@sliced peaches in 100% juice (15-ounce cans){2%cans}
@banana, peeled (optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Collect all ingredients and freeze cans before starting to prepare the recipe.

Place cans of fruit in freezer for at least 3 hours. Take out when fully frozen.*

Run frozen cans briefly under hot water, or let them sit on counter for 30 minutes to loosen contents. Remove fruit from the cans, and use a sharp knife to cut it into small pieces.

If using a banana, peel, and cut into slices. Banana does not need to be frozen.

Place frozen, chopped fruit (and banana, if using) in the blender, and blend until creamy.

Serve immediately.

Freeze any leftovers in a freezer-safe container or sealable plastic freezer bags.
