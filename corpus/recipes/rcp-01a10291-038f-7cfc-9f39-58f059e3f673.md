For best taste, refrigerate for at least 1 hour to allow flavors to blend. Serve over fish or lean meats, with hummus and pita, or as a stuffing inside fresh tomatoes. Materials: You will need a large bowl, measuring cups, measuring spoons, mixing spoon, and a sharp knife. Learn more about: Cucumbers Tomatoes Herbs Onions Lemons

-- Ingredients

@brown rice (cooked){3%cups}
@cucumber (chopped){3/4%cup}
@tomato (chopped){3/4%cup}
@fresh parsley (chopped){1/2%cup}
@fresh mint leaves (chopped){1/4%cup}
@green onions (sliced){1/4%cup}
@olive oil{1/4%cup}
@lemon juice{1/4%cup}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@freshly ground black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Combine rice, cucumber, tomato, parsley, mint, green onions, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in large bowl.

Toss well and chill.
