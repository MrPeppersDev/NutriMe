>> description: Jazz up this hot drink by adding cinnamon.

>> notes: You can substitute 1 cup skim milk for the reconstituted nonfat dried milk.

-- Ingredients

@nonfat dry milk{1/3%cup}
@water{1%cup}
@brewed coffee{1%cup}
@hot chocolate mix{4%tablespoons}
@lite whipped topping (optional){}
@cinnamon (optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Heat reconstituted non-fat dried milk in saucepan until warm; do not boil.

Add coffee and hot chocolate mix. Stir well and heat to desired temperature.

Divide coffee mixture between two mugs. Top with non-fat whipped topping and cinnamon, if desired.
