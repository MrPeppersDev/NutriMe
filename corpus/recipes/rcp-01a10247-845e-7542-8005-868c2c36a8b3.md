-- Ingredients

@scoops vanilla ice cream (or gelato){2}
@matcha{1%teaspoon}
@water{2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Make the thick matcha first by pouring 2 tablespoons of 175°F water (simmered water that's been slightly cooled down) into sifted matcha in a bowl. Mix well using a bamboo whisk or a spoon.

Scoop ice cream or gelato in a cup and pour thick matcha on top.

Serve immediately.
