-- Ingredients

@olive oil (or canola oil){2%teaspoons}
@onion (large, chopped){1}
@garlic powder{1%teaspoon}
@10-ounce packages of frozen sliced carrots{1 1/2}
@dry lentils (rinsed and drained){1%cup}
@diced tomatoes, low-sodium (14.5 ounces each){3%cans}
@water{3%cups}
@chili powder{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat.

Add chopped onion.

Cook for 3 minutes, or until tender.

Stir in garlic powder, carrots, lentils, tomatoes, water, and chili powder.

Simmer, uncovered, for about 20 minutes or until lentils are tender.
