-- Ingredients

@brown rice, uncooked{1 1/2%cups}
@water{3%cups}
@lemons, juiced{2}
@green onions (scallions){3}
@fresh parsley, minced{1/4%cup}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1%dash}
@olive oil (or cooking oil of choice){1/4%cup}
@mixed vegetables, cut into small pieces{1 1/2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash rice and put into a 2-quart pot with water. Bring to boil, then lower heat to simmer, and cook uncovered until all the water is absorbed (approximately 45 minutes)

Pour into medium sized bowl and allow to cool.

Pour lemon juice over rice and stir. When rice is cooled to lukewarm, add vegetables, salt, pepper, and oil.

Stir and chill at least 1 hour. This dish also can be served warm.
