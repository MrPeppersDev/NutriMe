-- Ingredients

@coconut water (or water){1/2%cup}
@small cucumber (or 1/2 large cucumber){1}
@spinach or kale (packed){1%cup}
@pineapple (frozen){1%cup}
@green apple (cored and sliced){1}
@Juice from one lemon{}
@Nub of fresh ginger (peeled (optional)){}

-- Instructions

Toss all of the ingredients into a blender.

Blend until smooth.

Serving in a glass.
