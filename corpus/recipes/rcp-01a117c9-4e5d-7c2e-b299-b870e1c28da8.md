-- Ingredients

@olive oil{2%tbsp}
@large onion, very finely diced{1}
@garlic, smashed and minced{3%cloves}
@ground veal{1/4%lb}
@ground pork{1/4%lb}
@ground beef{1/4%lb}
@eggs{2}
@grated Parmesan cheese{1/4%cup}
@fresh parsley, finely chopped{1/4%cup}
@dried basil{1/4%tsp}
@dried oregano{1/4%tsp}
@crushed red pepper{1/4%tsp}
@seasoned bread crumbs{1%cup}
@milk{1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.

Add the oil to a large pan and place over medium-high heat. Add the onions, season them with about a half teaspoon of salt, and cook for 2 minutes, or until translucent.

In a large bowl, combine the beef, pork, veal, cheese, herbs, and bread crumbs. Using your hands, combine the ingredients. Add the onions and season with another teaspoon or so of salt. Mix again, using your hands.

Shape the meatballs to whatever size you'd like. You can use an ice cream scoop if you find it important that all of your meatballs are the same size. I just whip it.

Oil a large pan and place over medium-high heat. Give the pan a few minutes to heat up. Add the meatballs, turning them occasionally to ensure that all sides of the meatball are browned. Add the meatballs, additional time.

Place the browned meatballs on a cookie sheet and bake for 18 minutes. Larger meatballs may require additional time.

Add to marinara sauce as it simmers, freeze for later, or eat them plain!
