-- Ingredients

@(14.5 ounces) green beans, low-sodium{1%can}
@cornstarch{1%teaspoon}
@vinegar{1/3%cup}
@brown sugar{1/4%cup}
@small onion, sliced{1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Drain the beans, and save the liquid from the can in small bowl.

Pour 1/2 cup bean liquid into the saucepan.

Add the cornstarch in the bean liquid. Stir well.

Add the vinegar and brown sugar.

Put on medium heat and bring to a boil.

Turn the heat to low.

Add the green beans and onions. Heat and serve.
