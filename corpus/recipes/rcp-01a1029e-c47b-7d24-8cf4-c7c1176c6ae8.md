-- Ingredients

@pasta (cooked){4%cups}
@broccoli (blanched, cut in pieces){2%cups}
@carrot (cooked, sliced){1%cup}
@red pepper (strips){1/2%cup}
@green onion (chopped){1/4%cup}
@Italian salad dressing, light or reduced fat (can use more if desired){1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix all ingredients together in a large bowl.

Refrigerate for about 30 minutes before serving.
