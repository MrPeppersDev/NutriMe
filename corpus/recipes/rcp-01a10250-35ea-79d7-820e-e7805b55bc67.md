>> title: Cilbir (Turkish Eggs with Yogurt Recipe)
>> servings: 1
>> source: https://www.anediblemosaic.com/cilbir-turkish-eggs-recipe/
>> attribution: anediblemosaic.com

-- Ingredients

@plain Greek yogurt (or Turkish yogurt if you can find it, or any type of thick yogurt (at room temperature)){1/2%cup}
@garlic (crushed){1%clove}
@salt{1/8%teaspoon}
@butter (salted or unsalted){1 1/2%tablespoons}
@extra-virgin olive oil{1/2%tablespoon}
@smoked sweet paprika ((see Notes to use Aleppo pepper instead)){1/4%teaspoon}
@cayenne pepper (or hot paprika (more or less to taste; see Notes to use Aleppo pepper instead)){1/8%teaspoon}
@large eggs (poached (see Notes for instructions)){2}
@chopped fresh dill (or 1/2 teaspoon dried dill weed){1 1/2%teaspoons}
@Salt and black pepper (to taste){}

-- Instructions

Stir together all ingredients in a small bowl. Set aside.

Melt the butter with the oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat. Add the spices and cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly. Set aside. (TIP: Now is a good time to make the poached eggs; see Notes for instructions.)

Spread the garlic yogurt out in a shallow bowl. Place the poached eggs on top. Drizzle on the spiced butter. Sprinkle on the dill. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
