-- Ingredients

@margarine, soft{1/2%cup}
@honey{1%cup}
@carrots, grated{1%cup}
@egg whites, beaten{2}
@all-purpose flour{2%cups}
@baking powder{2%teaspoons}
@baking soda{1/4%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@cinnamon{1%teaspoon}
@oatmeal, quick cooking, uncooked{2%cups}
@raisins{1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 °F.

In a large bowl, cream together margarine and honey. Stir in carrots and egg whites.

Stir together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, oatmeal and raisins. Gradually stir flour-oatmeal mixture into creamed mixture, just until all flour is mixed. Do not over mix.

Drop from teaspoon on greased baking sheet. Flatten slightly and bake at 350 °F for 10 minutes, or until lightly browned.
