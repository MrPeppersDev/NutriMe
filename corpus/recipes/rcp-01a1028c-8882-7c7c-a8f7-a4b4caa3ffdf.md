-- Ingredients

@flank steak{3%lbs}
@medium limes ((juice only)){2}
@lemon juice{1/4%cup}
@orange juice{1/4%cup}
@garlic powder{2%tsp}
@dried oregano{2%tsp}
@salt{1%tsp}
@black pepper{1%tsp}
@ground cumin{1%tsp}
@paprika{1%tsp}
@chili powder{1/2%tsp}
@Corn tortillas, pico de gallo, avocado, or other taco ingredients.{}

-- Instructions

In a bowl whisk together all ingredients (except flank steak).

Place flank steak in slow cooker, pour marinade over steak. Cook on HIGH for 4 hours, or LOW for 8 hours.

When steak is cooked through, shred with a fork. Serve with your favorite taco ingredients.
