Use low calorie salad dressing and leave out the vinegar and oil. Try a squeeze of lemon or lime juice in place of vinegar! -- Ingredients

@brown rice (cooked){1%cup}
@carrot (shredded){1}
@broccoli (chopped fine){1%cup}
@small red onion (chopped){1}
@tomato (chopped){1%cup}
@sweet bell pepper, green, red, or yellow (chopped){1}
@kidney beans, low-sodium (15.5 ounce, drained, rinsed){1%can}
@cilantro (or other fresh herbs) (chopped fine){2%tablespoons}
@red wine vinegar (or white or cider){2%tablespoons}
@vegetable oil{1%tablespoon}
@salt and pepper (to taste, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash and chop vegetables and mix with cooked rice.

In a small bowl, add vinegar, oil, dill, salt and pepper. (Or use your favorite dressing.) Pour over rice mixture.

Add beans and toss well. Serve cold and enjoy!
