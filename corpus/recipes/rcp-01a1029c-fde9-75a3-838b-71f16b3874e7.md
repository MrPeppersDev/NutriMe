-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil{2%tablespoons}
@garlic cloves (finely chopped){2}
@sweet potatoes (cut into 1-inch slices){3}
@corn cobs (cut into 2-inch sections){3}
@eggplant (cut into 1/2-inch slices){1}
@green onions (trimmed){12}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix oil and garlic in a large bowl. Add vegetables and toss.

Place vegetable on broiler pan or grill. Cook 10 minutes, turning twice until vegetables are tender.

Place vegetables on platter. Serve.
