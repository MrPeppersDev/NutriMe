-- Ingredients

@apple (medium, skinned and cored (not cut into pieces)){1}
@brown sugar{1%teaspoon}
@nutmeg{1/2%tablespoon}
@cinnamon{1%tablespoon}
@raisins{2%tablespoons}
@butter{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place apple on plate with opening facing up (cored apples should have bottom intact).

Stir sugar, nutmeg, and cinnamon in a separate cup, and sprinkle into and onto apple.

Sprinkle raisins on and around apple.

Drop butter alternative spread onto apple and cover with plastic wrap or wax paper.

Microwave on HIGH for 2 1/2 to 3 minutes.

Let sit outside of microwave for at least 30 seconds before eating. It will be HOT.
