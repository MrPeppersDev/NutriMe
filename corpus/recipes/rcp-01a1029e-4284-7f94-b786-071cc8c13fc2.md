-- Ingredients

@white rice, long-grain uncooked{1%cup}
@water{5%cups}
@milk, 1%{1/2%cup}
@vanilla extract{1/2%tablespoon}
@ground cinnamon{1/2%tablespoon}
@sugar{2/3%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Pour the rice and water into the bowl of a blender; blend until the rice just begins to break up, about one minute. Let rice and water stand at room temperature for about three hours.

Strain the rice water into a pitcher and discard the rice. Stir the milk, vanilla, cinnamon, and sugar into the rice water.

Chill and stir before serving over ice.
