-- Ingredients

@romaine lettuce, chopped{4%cups}
@red onion, chopped{1/4%cup}
@canned black beans, low-sodium (drained and rinsed){1%cup}
@orange, peeled and diced{1}
@tomato, diced{1}
@olive oil (or cooking oil of choice){1%tablespoon}
@red wine vinegar{3%tablespoons}
@oregano{1%teaspoon}
@black pepper (to taste, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toss all ingredients together in large salad bowl.

Serve immediately or refrigerate up to one hour.
