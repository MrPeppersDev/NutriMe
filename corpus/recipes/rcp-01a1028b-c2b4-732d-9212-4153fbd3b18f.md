>> notes: Chef's Notes: Use any of your favorite nuts in place of pecans. Or, leave them out if someone is allergic. Materials: You will need a large skillet, measuring cups, measuring spoons, mixing spoon, and a sharp knife. Learn more about: Apples Cranberries Celery Onions

-- Ingredients

@chopped pecans{4%ounces}
@canola oil (divided){4%teaspoons}
@diced celery{1 1/2%cups}
@diced onions{1 1/2%cups}
@medium red apple, such as Jonathan or Gala (halved, cored, and diced, about 1 1/2 cup total){1}
@hot cooked brown rice{2%cups}
@hot cooked wild rice{1%cup}
@dried cranberries{1/2%cup}
@medium jalapeno pepper (seeded (if desired) and finely chopped OR 1/4 tsp dried pepper flakes){1}
@chopped fresh sage (or 1 1/2 tsp dried rubbed sage){1 1/2%tablespoons}
@salt{3/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the pecans and cook 2-3 minutes or until beginning to brown, stirring frequently. Set aside on separate plate.

Heat 1 teaspoon of the oil over medium heat. Cook the celery and onion 8 minutes or until beginning to lightly brown on edge, stirring occasionally.

Add the apples and cook 4 minutes or until tender crisp.

Stir in the pecans and the remaining ingredients and cook 3-4 minutes or until the rice mixture is heated, stirring occasionally.
