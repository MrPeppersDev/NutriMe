-- Ingredients

@dough
@jam
@chocolate chips{}
@sugar

-- Instructions

Cut out heart shapes from dough.

Fill each heart shape with jam.

Add chocolate chips on top.

Fold the dough over the filling and seal the edges.

Poke a stick through the center of each pie.

Sprinkle sugar on top.

Bake until golden brown.
