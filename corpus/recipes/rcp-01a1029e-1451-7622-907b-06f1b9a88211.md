-- Ingredients

@chocolate chips{1%cup}
@14-oz Mori-Nu Firm Tofu{1}
@milk{1/4%cup}
@vanilla extract{2%tsp}
@ground cinnamon{1/4%tsp}
@Truvia sweetener{1%tsp}

-- Instructions

Melt chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl. Dump that into a food processor and then add in the rest of the ingredients.

Puree together until smooth. Pour into containers and refrigerate or eat right away (both ways are good!).
