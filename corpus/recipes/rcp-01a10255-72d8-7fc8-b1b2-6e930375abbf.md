-- Ingredients

@oats{3/4%cup}
@brown sugar{1%tbsp}
@granulated sugar{1%tbsp}
@molasses{1%tbsp}
@pumpkin pie spice{1/2%tsp}
@ginger{1/8%tsp}
@salt{1/8%tsp}
@milk{1%cup}

-- Instructions

Add all ingredients to a pint sized jar in the order listed. Put the lid on tightly and shake until combined thoroughly. Refrigerate 4 hours or overnight and serve cold or heat in the microwave for about 1 minute. Garnish with extra cinnamon, if desired.
