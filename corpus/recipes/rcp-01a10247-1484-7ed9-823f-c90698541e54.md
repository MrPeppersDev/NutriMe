>> title: High Protein Silken Tofu Banana Pudding (Vegan)
>> servings: 4
>> source: https://allyumcooks.com/high-protein-silken-tofu-banana-pudding-vegan/
>> attribution: allyumcooks.com

-- Ingredients

@(16 oz) block silken tofu, drained{1}
@coconut sugar{1/3%cup}
@vanilla extract{1/4%teaspoon}
@melted coconut oil (refined or unrefined){1%tablespoon}
@banana{1}
@1/4– 1/2 cup crushed graham crackers or vanilla wafers{}
@1–1 1/2 sliced bananas{}

-- Instructions

Drain the silken tofu and pat it dry to remove excess liquid.

In a blender or food processor, combine tofu, coconut sugar, vanilla extract, melted coconut oil, and one banana.

Blend until smooth and creamy, scraping down sides as needed.

Taste and adjust sweetness if necessary.

Layer pudding, crushed graham crackers or wafers, and banana slices in serving cups or a dish.

Repeat layers, finishing with pudding on top.

Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.
