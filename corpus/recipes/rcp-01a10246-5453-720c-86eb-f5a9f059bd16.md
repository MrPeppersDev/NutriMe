-- Ingredients

@Milk{1%Cup}
@Zested Orange{1/2}
@Heavy Cream{2/3%cup}
@Zested Orange{1/2}
@Dark Chocolate{2/3%cup}
@Spiced Chipotle Powder{1/4%teaspoon}
@shots Espresso{2}
@Spiced Dark Chocolate Ganache{2%Tablespoons}
@Orange Infused Milk{2/3%cup}
@Dehydrated Orange Slice (for garnish){1}

-- Instructions

Warm your milk to a light simmer. Add the orange zest and allow to infuse for 30 minutes. Remove the orange zest/peels, then chill the infused milk in the fridge until you’re ready to use it.

Add the cream & orange zest to a saucepan and bring to a simmer, not fully boiling. Meanwhile, combine the chocolate and chipotle powder in a bowl.

Pour the orange cream over the dark chocolate and chipotle powder. Stir to combine as the warm cream starts to melt the chocolate until smooth.

Add espresso and ganache into your mug and whisk until smooth.

Pour your hot, steamed infused milk on top. Feel free to use a milk frother with the hot milk if you don't have an espresso machine.

Garnish with a dehydrated orange slice if desired and enjoy!
