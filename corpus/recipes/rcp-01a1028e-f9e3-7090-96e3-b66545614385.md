-- Ingredients

@medium potatoes, sliced{2}
@carrots, sliced{2%cups}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@onion, sliced{1/2%cup}
@90% lean ground beef, browned and drained{1%pound}
@green beans{1 1/2%cups}
@low-sodium tomato soup{1%can}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Lightly oil or spray baking dish with cooking spray.

Layer ingredients in order given. Cover.

Bake at 350 degrees F for 45 minutes or until tender and thoroughly heated.

Uncover and bake 15 more minutes.
