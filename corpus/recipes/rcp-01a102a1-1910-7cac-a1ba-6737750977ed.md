-- Ingredients

@water{4 1/2%cups}
@light buttery spread{4 1/2%teaspoons}
@dehydrated potatoes{4 1/2%cups}
@low-fat milk{1/4%cup}
@garlic powder (optional){1/2%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Over medium to high heat, combine water and light buttery spread in a medium-sized pot, and bring it to a boil.

Turn off heat; add potatoes and mix well.

Add milk to potatoes and mix well. If using garlic powder, add that too.
