-- Ingredients

@avocado, diced{1}
@frozen corn kernels, thawed{3/4%cup}
@grape tomatoes, quartered{1/2%cup}
@fresh cilantro, chopped{1%tablespoon}
@lime juice{2%teaspoons}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toss avocado, corn, tomatoes, cilantro, lime juice, and salt in a medium bowl.

Chill one hour and then serve.
