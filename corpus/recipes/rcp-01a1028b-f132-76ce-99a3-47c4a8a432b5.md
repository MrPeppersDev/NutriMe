-- Ingredients

@acorn squashes, washed{4}
@butter (or margarine){1%tablespoon}
@apples, chopped{2}
@brown sugar, packed{2%teaspoons}
@cinnamon{1/2%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Cut squash into halves and remove seeds. Place in a glass dish, cover with plastic wrap and microwave on high for 5 minutes.

Melt butter in a separate bowl in the microwave. Mix in apples, sugar, and cinnamon. Microwave for 1 1/2 minutes.

Spoon apple filling into each squash half. Cover and microwave on high for 3-5 minutes until squash and apples are tender.

Serve warm.
