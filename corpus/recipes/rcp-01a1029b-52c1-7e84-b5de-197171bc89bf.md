>> title: Baked Chilean Sea Bass with Lemon Parmesan Cream Sauce
>> servings: 2
>> source: https://dudethatcookz.com/foil-baked-chilean-sea-bass-with-lemon-parmesan-cream-sauce/?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=SocialWarfare

-- Ingredients

@Chilean sea bass{1%lb}
@fresh leaf spinach{12%oz}
@heavy whipping cream{1/4%cup}
@minced garlic{2%tsp}
@black pepper{1%tsp}
@Creole seasoning{1%tsp}
@sea salt{1%tsp}
@butter{2%tbsp}
@butter{3%tbsp}
@all-purpose flour{2%tbsp}
@minced garlic{3%tsp}
@white wine{1/4%cup}
@chicken broth{1/2%cup}
@heavy whipping cream{1%cup}
@grated parmesan cheese{3%tbsp}
@garlic powder{1/2%tsp}
@oregano{1%tsp}
@black pepper{1/2%tsp}
@sea salt{1/4%tsp}
@lemon{1}

-- Instructions

Preheat oven to 400 degrees.

Season the sea bass on both sides with sea salt, black pepper, and Creole seasoning. Set to the side.

Heat a cooking pan to medium heat and add butter. Once the butter melts, add minced garlic and cook it for 1 minute. Add the spinach and pour heavy cream over the spinach. Cook spinach until it begins to wilt (but do not overcook as it will finish in the oven). Remove from heat.

On 2 large pieces of foil, add half of the slightly cooked spinach to the center of each piece. Place the sea bass over the spinach and seal the foil together so the fish can steam.

Bake the fish for 12 minutes on a baking sheet to prevent leakage. Set to the side when done.

While the fish is cooking, you can prepare the sauce. Heat a separate cooking pan to medium heat and add butter. Once the butter melts, add flour and minced garlic. Allow to cook for 1 to 2 minutes.

Add white wine and chicken broth and cook for 3 minutes. Add the heavy whipping cream, parmesan cheese, garlic powder, oregano, black pepper, and sea salt.

Zest the skin of the lemon into the sauce. Slice the lemon in half and squeeze both halves of juice into the sauce. Cook on medium-low heat until well blended.

Remove the cooked fish and spinach from the foil. Pour on the sauce.
