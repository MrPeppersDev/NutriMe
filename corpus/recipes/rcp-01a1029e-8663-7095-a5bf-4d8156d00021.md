-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of your choice){2%teaspoons}
@onion (chopped){1}
@ground ginger{1%teaspoon}
@turmeric{1/2%teaspoon}
@cumin{1/2%teaspoon}
@mixed vegetables (chopped cauliflower, peppers, carrots, or peas){2%cups}
@large baking potato (diced){1}
@white rice (uncooked){1%cup}
@water{2 1/2%cups}
@low-sodium kidney beans (15.5 ounces, drained, rinsed){1%can}
@salt (1/2 teaspoon, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

Add onion, ginger, turmeric, and cumin, stir while cooking for 1 minute.

Add remaining ingredients. Bring to a boil, cover, and reduce heat.

Simmer 20 to 25 minutes. Serve hot.

Refrigerate leftovers with in 2 to 3 hours.
