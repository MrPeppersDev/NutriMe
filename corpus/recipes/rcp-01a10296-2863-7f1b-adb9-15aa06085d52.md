-- Ingredients

@low-fat plain yogurt{1/4%cup}
@fat-free mayonnaise{1/4%cup}
@fat-free (skim) milk{1/4%cup}
@dried Italian seasoning{1/2%teaspoon}
@garlic, minced{1/2%clove}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a small bowl, combine all the above ingredients.

Refrigerate in a covered container for at least an hour. Serve chilled.
