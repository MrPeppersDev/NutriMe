-- Ingredients

@baby Dutch potatoes (1 bag){1 1/2%pounds}
@unsalted butter (or plant butter){1%tablespoon}
@olive oil{1%tablespoon}
@sea salt (divided use){1%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@granulated garlic{1/4%teaspoon}
@fresh chopped herbs (parsley, chives, thyme, oregano, chervil, rosemary, combination, dill.){2-3%tablespoons}
@grated Parmesan cheese (optional garnish){2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Place potatoes in a large pot like a Dutch oven and cover with an inch of cold water. Add 3/4 teaspoon salt to the water to season the potatoes and bring out their flavor. Bring the water almost a boil, turn down and simmer on medium-low until tender with pierced with a sharp knife tip, about 20 minutes.

When the potatoes are cooked to tender, drain in a colander or strainer. Add the potatoes back to the hot cooking pot to finish.

To finish potatoes, add the butter and oil to the pot over medium-low heat. When warm, add last 1/4 teaspoon of salt, the pepper, and garlic, then add potatoes and turn them over with a large spoon to coat. Toss with the herbs and serve.
