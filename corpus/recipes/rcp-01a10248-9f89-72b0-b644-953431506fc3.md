-- Ingredients

@unsalted butter{1/4%cup}
@all-purpose flour{1/3%cup}
@kosher salt{2%teaspoons}
@dry mustard (or Dijon mustard){1/2%teaspoon}
@paprika{3/8%teaspoon}
@whole milk (hot){1 1/3%cup}
@heavy cream (hot){1 1/3%cup}
@sharp Cheddar cheese (grated ){3/4%pound}
@Worcestershire sauce, (or Annie's Vegan Worcestershire Sauce to keep it vegetarian){1%tablespoon}
@pinch cayenne pepper{}

-- Instructions

Melt the butter in a heavy bottomed pot, such as a Le Creuset, over medium-low heat. A large, deep skillet also works very well. Add the flour, salt, dry mustard, and paprika to the pan and stir with a wooden spoon until it forms a paste. Cook for 2-3 minutes.

Heat the milk in the microwave oven (or on the stovetop) until it's hot, and gradually whisk it into the paste. It's important to keep whisking the mixture. Heat the heavy cream in the microwave oven (or on the stovetop) until it's hot, and gradually whisk it into the sauce. It's important to keep a constant whisking. Cook for 8-10 minutes to allow it to thicken, occasionally whisking.

Add the grated cheddar cheese and fold it into the sauce using a silicone spatula until the cheese is completely melted. A luscious, smooth sauce developed after 2-3 minutes.

Stir in the Worcestershire sauce. Finish with a pinch of cayenne.
