-- Ingredients

@cauliflower florets (fresh or frozen, thawed){3%cups}
@broccoli florets (fresh or frozen, thawed){2%cups}
@red kidney beans, low-sodium (15 1/2 ounce, drained){1%can}
@carrots, sliced diagonally (medium){2}
@olives, large pitted (sliced){1%cup}
@green onion (sliced){2}
@red wine vinegar{1/4%cup}
@vegetable oil (or olive oil){1/4%cup}
@cilantro or parsley (chopped){1%tablespoon}
@garlic (minced){1%clove}
@sugar{1%teaspoon}
@dried basil (1/2 teaspoon, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In large bowl, combine all salad ingredients.

In a small jar with a tight fitting lid, combine all dressing ingredients and shake well.

Pour dressing over vegetables and stir to coat vegetables. Cover; refrigerate 1 to 4 hours to marinate, tossing occasionally.
