-- Ingredients

@chickpeas{1%can}
@natural peanut butter{1/2%cup}
@maple syrup{1/3%cup}
@vanilla{2%tsp}
@salt{1/2%tsp}
@baking powder{1/4%tsp}
@baking soda{1/4%tsp}
@dark chocolate chips{}
@@traderjoes dye-free sprinkles{}

-- Instructions

blend all ingredients together

spread into baking dish + sprinkle with choc chips/sprinkles (optional)

& bake at 350 for 25 mins
