-- Ingredients

@cabbage head (shredded - 2 quarts){1/2}
@fruit cocktail (15 ounces, juice packed, drained){1%can}
@yogurt, non-fat plain{1%cup}
@lemon{1}
@honey{1/2%cup}
@vinegar{1/4%cup}
@banana (sliced){1}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/8%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Shred cabbage.

Combine all ingredients in a large bowl.
