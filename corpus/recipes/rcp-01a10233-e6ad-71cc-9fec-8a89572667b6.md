-- Ingredients

@Granny Smith apple, rinsed and sliced thinly (with skin){1}
@lemon juice{2%Tbsp}
@mixed lettuce greens (or your favorite lettuce) (about 5 C), rinsed{1%bag}
@dried cranberries{1/2%C}
@walnuts, chopped{1/4%C}
@unsalted sunflower seeds{1/4%C}
@low-fat raspberry vinaigrette dressing{1/3%C}

-- Instructions

Sprinkle lemon juice on the apple slices.

Mix the lettuce, cranberries, apple, walnuts, and sunflower seeds in a bowl.

Toss with raspberry vinaigrette dressing, to lightly cover the salad, and serve.
