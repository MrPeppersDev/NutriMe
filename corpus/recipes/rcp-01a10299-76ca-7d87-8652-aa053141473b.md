-- Ingredients

@peanut butter{1%tablespoon}
@graham cracker (large, or use whole grain or saltine crackers){1}
@cheddar cheese (shredded){1%tablespoon}
@raisins{3}
@peas (large green){2}
@grape (cut in half){1/2}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Spread peanut butter on a cracker in the shape of a face.

Sprinkle grated cheese at top for hair.

Place raisins on cracker for mouth.

Use peas for eyes and 1/2 grape for nose.
