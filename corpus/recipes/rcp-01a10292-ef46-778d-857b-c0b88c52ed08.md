-- Ingredients

@onion, chopped{1}
@large carrots, chopped{3}
@celery stalks, chopped{3}
@garlic, finely chopped{1/2%tablespoon}
@vegetable oil{2%tablespoons}
@hominy (2 - 15 ounce cans){2 1/2%cups}
@chicken (about 12 ounces){1%can}
@water{4%cups}
@cayenne or jalapeño chilies, seeds removed and finely chopped (optional){1%teaspoon}
@chili powder (optional){1/2%teaspoon}
@oregano (optional){1/2%teaspoon}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@pepper{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

In a medium-sized pot, brown onion, carrots, celery, and garlic in vegetable oil over medium heat for 10 minutes.

Add hominy, chicken, and water. If using cayenne pepper, jalapeño chilies, chili powder, or oregano, add that, too. Mix well.

Bring pot to a boil. Lower heat and cook for about 10 minutes.

Add salt and pepper. Serve hot.
