-- Ingredients

@grapefruit, peeled{1}
@orange, peeled{1}
@fresh greens (lettuce){10%cups}
@small red onion, sliced thin{1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Cut fruit into bite size pieces.

Toss with lettuce and onion. Mix remaining ingredients for dressing. Drizzle over salad and toss just before serving.
