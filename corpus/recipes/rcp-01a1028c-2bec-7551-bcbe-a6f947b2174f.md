>> servings: 4
>> source: https://themodernproper.com/posts/heirloom-blt-with-pesto
>> attribution: themodernproper.com

-- Ingredients

@ciabatta bread{8%slices}
@mayonnaise{1/4%cup}
@basil pesto, homemade or store-bought{1/2%cup}
@thick bacon, cooked{8%slices}
@arugula{2%cups}
@large heirloom tomato, sliced{1}
@large eggs, fried to your preference{4}

-- Instructions

Position a rack in the upper third of the oven. Turn the oven to broil. Place the bread directly on the oven rack and broil for 1 minute or until toasted.

Evenly spread the mayonnaise on one side of 4 pieces of bread. Spread 2 tablespoons of the pesto on one side of each of the remaining pieces of bread.

Layer the bacon, arugula, tomato, and eggs on the pesto-topped bread. Top with the remaining slices of bread and serve.
