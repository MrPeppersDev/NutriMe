>> servings: 4
>> source: http://shewearsmanyhats.com/egg-drop-soup-recipe/
>> attribution: shewearsmanyhats.com

-- Ingredients

@chicken broth{32%ounces}
@ground ginger{1%teaspoon}
@soy sauce{1%tablespoon}
@eggs, beaten{2}
@chopped green onions (about 2 green onions){1/4%cup}
@salt and pepper{}

-- Instructions

Add chicken broth, ground ginger and soy sauce to a saucepan; bring to a simmer.

Slowly stream in beaten eggs while stirring the soup in one direction.

Add green onions. Salt and pepper to taste.
