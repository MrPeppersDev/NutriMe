-- Ingredients

@chicken breast halves (small, skin and bone removed){8}
@bread crumbs (Italian seasoned, crushed){1/2%cup}
@mozzarella cheese, part skim (shredded){8%ounces}
@canola oil{1%tablespoon}
@garlic (minced){4%cloves}
@spinach leaves, 10 ounces each{2%packages}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 400 °F.

Place chicken between 2 sheets of plastic wrap; pound chicken to 1/4 inch thick.

Coat chicken with bread crumbs and place on foil-covered baking sheet. Discard remaining bread crumbs. Bake 10 minutes.

Top chicken with cheese; bake 4 to 5 minutes until cheese is melted and chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165 °F.

Meanwhile, heat oil in a large skillet on medium heat. Add garlic and stir 1 minute.

Add half the spinach and cook 1 minute.

Add remaining spinach. Cook until wilted, turning occasionally. Serve with chicken.
