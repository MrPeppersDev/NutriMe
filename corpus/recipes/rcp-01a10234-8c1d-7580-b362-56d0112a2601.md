-- Ingredients

@green seedless grapes, rinsed{48}
@red seedless grapes, rinsed{48}
@6-inch wooden skewers{}

-- Instructions

Thread six grapes, alternating grape colors, onto each wooden skewer.

Place skewers into the freezer for 30 minutes, or until frozen.

Serve immediately.
