-- Ingredients

@salt{1/4%tablespoon}
@small potatoes (1 pound){5}
@radish (1/2 sliced, optional){}
@basil (dried){1/2%teaspoon}
@thyme (dried){1/4%teaspoon}
@onion powder{1/4%teaspoon}
@yogurt, non-fat plain{3%tablespoons}
@mayonnaise, reduced calorie{1%tablespoon}
@prepared mustard{1 1/2%teaspoons}
@garlic (chopped){1/2%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Scrub potatoes and cut into cubes.

Place in medium saucepan and cover with water. Bring to a boil.

Cover, reduce heat, and simmer 12 minutes or until potatoes are done. Drain.

Mix dressing ingredients.

Combine hot potatoes, dressing, radishes, basil, thyme, and onion powder. Serve hot or cold.
