-- Ingredients

@milk (low-fat 1%){2%cups}
@fresh pitted mango (or 4 Tablespoons of frozen mango juice){1}
@banana (medium){1}
@ice cubes{2}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Put all ingredients into a blender.

Blend until foamy.

Serve immediately.
