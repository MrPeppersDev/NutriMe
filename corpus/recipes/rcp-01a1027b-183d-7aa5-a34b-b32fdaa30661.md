-- Ingredients

@medium cucumber (peeled){1}
@frozen peaches{2%cups}
@lemon (peeled){1}
@orange (peeled){1}
@inch piece of fresh ginger (peeled){1}
@apple (cored){1}
@frozen strawberries{1/3%cup}
@cold water{1%cup}
@ice{2%cups}

-- Instructions

Add all ingredients except ice to a high speed blender and blend until smooth.

Add ice and blend again until smooth

Yields 50 0z
