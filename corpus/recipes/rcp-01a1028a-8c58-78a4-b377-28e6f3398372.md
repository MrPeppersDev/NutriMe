-- Ingredients

@olive oil{2%teaspoons}
@scallions (chopped){1%cup}
@garlic (minced){3%cloves}
@small tomatoes (chopped){2}
@cilantro (minced){2%tablespoons}
@chuck beef stew meat (cut into small chunks){1 1/2%lb}
@all purpose flour (or gluten-free flour){2%tablespoons}
@light beer (I use Corona light or Corona 0){1/3%cup}
@water{1/3%cup}
@cumin{1/2%teaspoon}
@adobo seasoning (or seasoned salt){1/2%teaspoon}
@ground achiote{1/2%teaspoon}
@dry bay leaf{1}
@kosher salt ( or more to taste){1}
@baby red potatoes (halved or quartered){10%ounces}
@Aji Picante (for serving){}

-- Instructions

Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add 1 teaspoon of oil, scallions and garlic and sauté about 2 - 3 minutes; add tomatoes, cilantro and a pinch of salt. Cook another 2 minutes, then transfer to the slow cooker.

Wipe the skillet, add remaining oil and increase the heat to medium high.

Add the beef and brown on all side, about 4 minutes.

Season with 3/4 teaspoon salt and sprinkle flour over the meat, cook 1 minute turning often, then add to the slow cooker.

Add the beer, water, cumin, adobo, achiote, bay leaf and 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt.

Add potatoes and cover, cook on low heat 6 to 8 hours or until beef is tender and the potatoes are cooked through. Taste for salt, and adjust as needed.
