-- Ingredients

@red apples (such as Cameo Apples){2}
@celery stalks (diced){2}
@chicken breasts (skinless, cooked, diced, about 2 cups){2}
@plain non-fat Greek yogurt{1/4%cup}
@raisins{1/2%cup}
@mayonnaise{1/4%cup}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@ground black pepper{1/8%teaspoon}
@lettuce leaves (Bibb, Romaine, green or red leaf){16}

-- Instructions

Cut apples in quarters; remove core and chop.

In a medium size bowl, mix all ingredients, except lettuce.

To serve, arrange lettuce on serving plates top with apple and chicken salad.
