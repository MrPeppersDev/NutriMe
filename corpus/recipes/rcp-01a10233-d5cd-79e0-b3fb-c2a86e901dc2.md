>> title: Asian-Style Steamed Salmon
>> servings: 4
>> source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living/healthy-foods/healthy-eating-recipes/asian-style-steamed-salmon
>> attribution: NHLBI Delicious Heart Healthy Eating

-- Ingredients

@low-sodium chicken broth{1%C}
@shiitake mushroom caps, rinsed and sliced (or substitute dried shiitake mushrooms){1/2%C}
@fresh ginger, minced (or 2 tsp ground){2%Tbsp}
@scallions (green onions), rinsed and chopped{1/4%C}
@lite soy sauce{1%Tbsp}
@sesame oil (optional){1%Tbsp}
@salmon fillet, cut into 4 portions (3 oz each){12%oz}

-- Instructions

Combine chicken broth, mushroom caps, ginger, scallions, soy sauce, and sesame oil (optional) in a large, shallow sauté pan. Bring to a boil over high heat, then lower heat and simmer for 2–3 minutes.

Add salmon fillets, and cover with a tight-fitting lid. Cook gently over low heat for 4–5 minutes or until the salmon flakes easily with a fork in the thickest part (to a minimum internal temperature of 145 °F).

Serve one piece of salmon with ¼ cup of broth.
