-- Ingredients

@rotini or macaroni pasta (1 package){1%pound}
@chicken (about 12 ounces){1%can}
@low-sodium tomato sauce (about 15 ounces){1%can}
@low-sodium green peas, drained (about 8 ounces){1/2%can}
@pasteurized processed American cheese{3%slices}

-- Instructions

Preheat oven to 350 °F.

Cook rotini or macaroni according to package directions. Drain well.

In a large oven-safe dish, mix the chicken, tomato sauce, peas, and macaroni.

Add the 3 slices of cheese on top of the casserole.

Bake for 30 to 40 minutes.

Serve hot.
