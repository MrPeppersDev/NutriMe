-- Ingredients

@Sourdough loaf (or your favorite toasty bread){}
@Burrata
@Mikes hot honey{}
@Pistachios
@Olive oil{}

-- Instructions

Coat toast in olive oil and cook 2-3 min per side in a skillet

Spread 1/2 a ball of burrata cheese on toast

Drizzle honey overtop

Meanwhile, roughly crush pistachios and add to the top. Enjoy!
