-- Ingredients

@macaroni, cooked{1/2%cup}
@onion, finely chopped{1/4%cup}
@cucumber, finely chopped{1/2%cup}
@green pepper, diced{1/4%cup}
@vinegar{1%tablespoon}
@vegetable oil{1/2%tablespoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@parsley, chopped (optional){1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

In a medium-sized bowl, combine macaroni, onions, cucumbers, and green peppers. Mix well.

In a separate bowl, mix vinegar, vegetable oil, salt, and pepper. If using parsley, add that too.

Pour the prepared dressing over the pasta. Mix well.

Cover bowl and refrigerate for 30 to 45 minutes. Serve cold.
