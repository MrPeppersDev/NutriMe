-- Ingredients

@chicken broth, low-sodium (low sodium, 14.5 oz){3/4%can}
@water{1/2%cup}
@mixed vegetables (frozen, for soup){2%cups}
@tomatoes, low-sodium (14.5 oz, broken up){1%can}
@beef (cooked and diced){4%ounces}
@thyme leaves (crushed){1%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1%dash}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@bay leaf{1}
@noodles (narrow-width, uncooked){1 1/4%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water

Heat broth and water. Add vegetables, meat, and seasonings. Bring to boil. Reduce heat and boil gently, uncovered, for 15 minutes.

Add noodles. Cook until noodles are tender, about 10 minutes

Remove bay leaf. Serve.
