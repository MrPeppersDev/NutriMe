-- Ingredients

@bananas, sliced lengthwise{6}
@packed brown sugar{6%tablespoons}
@margarine or butter{4%tablespoons}
@raisins, or other dried chopped fruit{1/2%cup}
@chopped pecans{1/3%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 °F.

Lightly grease a 9x13 inch pan.

Arrange the split bananas in the pan.

Top evenly with brown sugar, small pieces of margarine, raisins, and pecans.

Bake for 30 minutes.
