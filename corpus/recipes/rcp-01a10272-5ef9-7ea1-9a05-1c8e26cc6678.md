-- Ingredients

@seedy bread (or whatever kind you like!){1%slice}
@butter (optional){1%tablespoon}
@whole milk ricotta cheese{2%tablespoons}
@heaping teaspoon local honey{1}
@chopped pistachios{1%tablespoon}
@kosher salt and coarse black pepper{}

-- Instructions

Toast bread.

Slather with butter if using and ricotta cheese. Sprinkle with pistachios, drizzle with honey and finish with a pinch of salt and pepper to taste.

Devour.
