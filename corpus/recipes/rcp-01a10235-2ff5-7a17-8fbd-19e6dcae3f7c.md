-- Ingredients

@tomato paste{5%Tbsp}
@ketchup{1%tsp}
@honey{2%tsp}
@molasses{1%tsp}
@Worcestershire sauce{1%tsp}
@white vinegar{4%tsp}
@cayenne pepper{3/4%tsp}
@ground black pepper{1/8%tsp}
@onion powder{1/4%tsp}
@grated ginger{1/8%tsp}
@garlic, minced{2%cloves}
@skinless chicken (4 breasts, 4 drumsticks){8%pieces}

-- Instructions

Combine all ingredients except chicken in a saucepan. Simmer for 15 minutes.

Place chicken on a large platter and brush it with half the sauce mixture.

Cover with plastic wrap and marinate in refrigerator for 1 hour.

Place chicken on a baking sheet lined with aluminum foil, and broil for 10 minutes on each side to seal in juices.

Remove from oven and brush chicken pieces with remaining sauce. Cover with aluminum foil and bake at 350 °F for 30 minutes.
