-- Ingredients

@Jamaican jerk seasoning ((store bought OR use the recipe below)){1%tablespoon}
@large chicken breasts (pounded to 1/2 inch thickness){3}
@slaw (or shredded lettuce, (I used red and green cabbage, thinly sliced)){2%cups}
@fresh cilantro leaves (roughly chopped){2%tablespoons}
@taco sized soft tortillas{8}
@lime wedges (for serving){}
@mayo{1/3%cup}
@Jamaican jerk seasoning{2%teaspoons}
@garlic powder{1%tablespoon}
@cayenne pepper{2-3%teaspoons}
@onion powder{2%teaspoons}
@dried parsley{2%teaspoons}
@sugar{2%teaspoons}
@salt{2%teaspoons}
@paprika{1%teaspoon}
@ground allspice{1%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/2%teaspoon}
@dried crushed red pepper{1/2%teaspoon}
@ground nutmeg{1/2%teaspoon}
@ground cinnamon{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Season chicken breasts with jerk seasoning, rubbing into both sides. Cook over medium heat on the stove for 4-5 minutes on each side until cooked through. Slice into strips.

Whisk together mayo and 2 teaspoons jerk seasoning.

Assemble tacos. Place slaw/lettuce on each tortilla. Top with chicken, then drizzle with sauce. Top with chopped cilantro. Serve with fresh lime wedges for drizzling on tacos.
