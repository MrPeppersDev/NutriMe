-- Ingredients

@water{1%cup}
@honey{1/2%cup}
@fresh sage leaves{10}
@bourbon{3%oz}
@honey sage syrup{3%tbsp}
@orange peel (for garnish){1}
@fresh sage leaves (for garnish ){1-2}

-- Instructions

Bring the water, honey, and sage to a boil in a pot over medium high heat. Reduce to low and simmer for 3 minutes. Turn off heat and let syrup cool.

Stir bourbon and cooled syrup with ice in a mixing glass. Fill lowball glass with ice and pour mix over. Twist your orange peel directly over glass to release oils. Garnish with a fresh sage leaf and orange peel. For a sweeter taste add more honey sage syrup.
