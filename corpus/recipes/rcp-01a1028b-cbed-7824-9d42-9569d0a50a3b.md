-- Ingredients

@lite mayonnaise-type dressing (or light mayonnaise){2%tablespoons}
@orange juice (1 tablespoon){1/16%cup}
@apple, diced{2%cups}
@celery, diced{1%cup}
@raisins{1/2%cup}
@nuts{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix orange juice with salad dressing or mayonnaise.

Toss apples, celery, raisins and nuts with the dressing mixture.
