>> title: Microwave Denver Scramble Slider
>> servings: 1
>> source: https://www.myplate.gov/recipes/microwave-denver-scramble-slider
>> attribution: USDA MyPlate Kitchen
>> description: This quick egg scramble sandwich is made fast in the microwave for a great on-the-go breakfast, lunch, or snack.
>> calories_per_serving: 240.00
>> original_contributor: Meeting Your MyPlate Goals on a Budget MyPlate National Strategic Partners Toolkit
>> notes: Don't overcook. Scrambled eggs will continue to cook and firm up after removed from microwave. Microwave ovens vary. Cook time may need to be adjusted. Learn more about: Bell Peppers Onions

-- Ingredients

@red or green bell peppers (chopped){2%tablespoons}
@onion (chopped){1%tablespoon}
@egg{1}
@thin slice deli ham (chopped){1}
@water{1%tablespoon}
@slider-sized bun or whole wheat English muffin (split and toasted){1}

-- Instructions

Place peppers and onions in 8 ounce ramekin or custard cup, or in a small bowl. Microwave on high 30 seconds; stir. Add egg, ham, and water, beat until egg is blended.

Microwave on high 30 seconds; stir. Microwave until egg is almost set, 30 to 45 seconds longer. Season with salt and pepper, if desired.

Serve in bun.
