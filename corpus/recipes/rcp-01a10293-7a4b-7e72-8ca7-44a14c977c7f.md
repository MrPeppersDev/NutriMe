-- Ingredients

@thin noodles, cooked and drained{12%oz}
@soy sauce{1/4%c}
@sugar{2%tbsp}
@garlic, minced{4%cloves}
@rice vinegar{2%tbsp}
@pure sesame oil{3%tbsp}
@hot chili oil{1/2%tsp}
@canola oil{4%tbsp}
@whole green onions, sliced thin{4}

-- Instructions

Whisk all ingredients (except noodles and green onion) together in a bowl. Taste and adjust ingredients as needed.

Pour sauce over warm noodles and toss to coat.

Sprinkle with green onion and toss.
