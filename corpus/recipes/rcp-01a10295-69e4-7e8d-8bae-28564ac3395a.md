-- Ingredients

@ears of corn (about 3 cups){6}
@chopped red onion{2%tablespoons}
@lemon juice{2%tablespoons}
@sugar{1/8%teaspoon}
@olive oil (or cooking oil of choice){2%tablespoons}
@chopped fresh basil (or 1 tsp dried basil){1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Cook corn in boiling water or in the microwave.

Cut corn from the cob. Add onion.

In a small bowl, whisk together lemon juice and sugar. Add oil and combine.

Stir the oil/lemon mixture and basil. And salt and pepper to taste (optional).

Refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
