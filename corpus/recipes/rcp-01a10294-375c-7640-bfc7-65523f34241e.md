-- Ingredients

@sugar{1/4%cup}
@ground cinnamon{1%teaspoon}
@margarine, tub-style{1/4%cup}
@square, whole-grain corn cereal{1 1/2%cups}
@square, whole-grain rice cereal{1 1/2%cups}
@square, whole-grain wheat cereal{1 1/2%cups}
@raisins or dried cranberries{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

In small bowl, mix sugar and cinnamon; set aside.

In large microwavable bowl, microwave butter uncovered on HIGH about 40 seconds, or until melted.

Stir in cereals until evenly coated. Microwave uncovered 2 minutes, stirring after 1 minute.

Sprinkle half of the sugar mixture evenly over cereals; stir. Sprinkle with remaining sugar mixture; stir.

Microwave uncovered 1 minute.

Stir in raisins or dried cranberries. Spread on paper towels to cool.
