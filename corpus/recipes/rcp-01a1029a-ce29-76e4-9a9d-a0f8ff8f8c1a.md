-- Ingredients

@medium banana{1}
@fresh peaches (or strawberries){1%cup}
@(8 ounce) container low-fat vanilla yogurt{1}
@100% fruit juice{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Put all ingredients in a blender.

Blend on high until smooth.

Pour into 2 glasses. Serve right away.
