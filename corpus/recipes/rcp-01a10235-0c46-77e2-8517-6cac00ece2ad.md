-- Ingredients

@For salad: 1 large mango, peeled and diced{}
@fresh blueberries{2%C}
@bananas, sliced{2}
@fresh strawberries, halved{2%C}
@seedless grapes{2%C}
@nectarines, peeled and sliced{2}
@kiwi fruit, peeled and sliced{1}

-- Instructions

Mix the fruit together in a large bowl.

In a small bowl, combine all the ingredients for the honey orange sauce and mix well.

Just before serving, pour the honey orange sauce over the fruit.
