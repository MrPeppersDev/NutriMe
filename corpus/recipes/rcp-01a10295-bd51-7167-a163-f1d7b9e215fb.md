>> title: Homemade Fruit and Vegetable Snacks
>> servings: 24
>> source: http://thestayathomechef.com/homemade-fruit-and-vegetable-snacks/
>> attribution: thestayathomechef.com

-- Ingredients

@orange juice{1/2%cup}
@baby spinach{2%cups}
@strawberries (hulled){2%cups}
@blueberries{1%cup}
@to 1/2 cup agave syrup{1/4}
@vanilla extract{1%teaspoon}
@(0.25 ounce) envelopes unflavored gelatin (about 5 tablespoons){5}

-- Instructions

Pour 1/2 cup orange juice into a blender. Add 2 cups baby spinach and blend until smooth.

Add 2 cups strawberries and 1 cup blueberries. Blend until smooth.

Pour the fruit mixture into a medium saucepan. Add 1/4 to 1/2 cup agave syrup, 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, and 5 (0.25 ounce) envelopes unflavored gelatin.

Cook over medium-high heat, whisking constantly, until the gelatin is completely dissolved, about 3 to 5 minutes.

Line a 13x17-inch rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper. Pour the fruit mixture evenly into the pan.

Let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or until set.

Cut into squares or use cookie cutters to create fun shapes. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
