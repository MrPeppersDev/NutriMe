-- Ingredients

@hearty whole grain bread (I love Dave's seeded varieties){2%slices}
@country Dijon (or sub Carolina mustard BBQ sauce){1-2%tsp}
@sprinkle everything seasoning + ground black pepper (or sub all-purpose seasoning such as Hawaiian rub with garlic and paprika){}
@deli sliced smoked turkey{2%oz}
@cooked turkey bacon (heated on stovetop or in toaster oven until crispy, then torn into small pieces){1%slice}
@- 1 oz sliced sharp white cheddar{1/2}
@- 1/3 Fuji apple, thinly sliced{1/4}
@sliced pickled red onion{}
@prepared coleslaw (or sub baby greens){2-3%Tbsp}
@- 1 oz ripe avocado{1/2}

-- Instructions

Spread Dijon/mustard sauce on one slice of bread. Smash avocado on the top slice. Sprinkle each with everything seasoning and pepper (or your favorite all-purpose seasoning). On top of the mustard slice, layer turkey, torn bacon, cheddar, apple, pickled onion, and slaw or greens. Top with the other slice of bread, avocado-side-down. Slice in half and enjoy!
