-- Ingredients

@whole wheat bread, cubed (or white bread or buns, dry){10%slices}
@water{1/3%cup}
@onion (chopped){1/2%cup}
@celery (chopped){1/2%cup}
@parsley, dried (or 1 Tbsp fresh parsley chopped){1%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@milk, fat free{1 1/2%cups}
@egg (lightly beaten){1}
@apples (optional - medium, pared, cored and chopped, or 1/4 cup raisins){2}
@raisins (optional, 1/4 cup){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 °F.

Put cubes in a large bowl. Set aside.

Put water in medium saucepan. Add onion, celery, parsley, salt, and pepper. Cook for 5 minutes. Do not drain. Pour over bread cubes.

Stir in milk and egg. Gently stir in apples, and raisins, if desired.

Spoon into a greased 2-quart baking dish.

Bake at 350 °F for 1 hour.
