-- Ingredients

@ground beef{1/2%pound}
@water{2%cups}
@low-sodium whole kernel corn (about 15 ounces){1%can}
@low-sodium cream style corn (about 30 ounces){2%cans}
@onions, diced{1%cup}
@garlic, finely chopped{1%tablespoon}
@chili powder{1%tablespoon}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/2%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

In a large pot, brown ground beef over medium to high heat for 8 to 10 minutes. Drain off fat.

Add water, corn kernels, cream style corn, onion, garlic, chili powder, salt, and pepper to pot. Mix well.

Cook over low heat for about 15 minutes. Serve hot.
