-- Ingredients

@cooked small pasta{2%cups}
@canned chicken, drained{1 1/2%cups}
@diced bell pepper{1%cup}
@sliced green onion{1/2%cup}
@shredded yellow squash{1%cup}
@canned corn kernels, low-sodium, drained{1/2%cup}
@frozen peas, thawed{1/2%cup}
@(15 ounces) black beans, low-sodium, rinsed and drained{1%can}
@fat-free Italian dressing{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Cook pasta according to package directions, drain; rinse.

Combine first eight ingredients (pasta through black beans) in a large bowl.

Toss gently with salad dressing.

Chill for several hours to blend flavors.
