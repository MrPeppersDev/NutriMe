-- Ingredients

@spinach, fresh{4%cups}
@romaine lettuce{4%cups}
@green pepper, chopped (or red, yellow, or orange peppers){2%cups}
@cherry tomatoes{2%cups}
@broccoli, chopped{1%cup}
@cauliflower, chopped{1%cup}
@yellow squash, sliced{1%cup}
@cucumber, sliced{1%cup}
@carrot, chopped{2%cups}
@zucchini, sliced{1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash all of the vegetables and mix them together in a large mixing bowl.

Top this colorful meal with the nonfat or low-fat dressing of your choice.
