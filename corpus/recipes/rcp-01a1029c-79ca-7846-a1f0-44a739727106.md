-- Ingredients

@frozen green beans (16 oz bag){3 1/2%cups}
@small potatoes (washed, peeled, and halved){8}
@medium spring onion/scallion (chopped){1}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Add all ingredients to a large stock pot and cover with water.

Bring to a boil and reduce to medium heat.

Cook until green beans and potatoes are tender.
