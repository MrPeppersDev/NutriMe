-- Ingredients

@low-fat plain yogurt (or low-fat sour cream){1/2%cup}
@(1 tablespoon) pickle relish{1/16%cup}
@onion, minced (or 2 teaspoons dried minced onion){1%tablespoon}
@dried parsley (or 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley){1%teaspoon}
@mustard{1%teaspoon}
@drops hot pepper sauce (optional){2}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a small bowl, combine all the ingredients.

Serve immediately or cover and refrigerate until used.
