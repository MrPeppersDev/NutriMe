>> attribution: NHLBI Delicious Heart Healthy Eating

-- Ingredients

@fresh yucca (cassava), cut into 3-inch sections and peeled (or 1 pound peeled frozen yucca){1%pound}
@Nonstick cooking spray{}

-- Instructions

In a kettle, combine the yucca with enough cold water to cover it by 1 inch. Bring the water to a boil, and slowly simmer the yucca for 20 to 30 minutes, or until it is tender.

Preheat oven to 350° F.

Transfer the yucca with a slotted spoon to a cutting board, let it cool, and cut it lengthwise into ¾-inch-wide wedges, discarding the thin woody core.

Spray a cookie sheet lightly with the cooking spray. Spread yucca wedges in a single layer on the cookie sheet, and spray the wedges with cooking spray. Cover with foil and bake for 8 minutes. Uncover and return to oven to bake for an additional 7 minutes.
