-- Ingredients

@ripe avocados{2}
@sour cream{1/2%cup}
@fresh cilantro{1%cup}
@garlic{1%clove}
@juice of 1 lime{}
@pinch onion powder{}
@salt{1/4%tsp}

-- Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a small food processor.

Blend until smooth

Chill to let flavors combine

Use as a dipping sauce or topping for your favorite foods.
