-- Ingredients

@a small (6-pound) seedless watermelon, rind removed, flesh cut into 1/4-inch slices{0.25}
@large orange, peel and pith removed, flesh cut into segments{1}
@small red onion, thinly sliced{0.25}
@feta cheese, thinly sliced{2%ounces}
@fresh parsley leaves{0.25%cup}
@extra-virgin olive oil{4%teaspoons}
@Coarse salt and ground pepper{}

-- Instructions

On a serving platter, arrange watermelon, orange, red onion, feta cheese, and parsley.

Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
