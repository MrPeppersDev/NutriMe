-- Ingredients

@(15 ounces) fruit cocktail, drained{1%can}
@bananas, sliced{2}
@oranges, cut into bite-size pieces{2}
@apples, cut into bite-size pieces{2}
@(8 ounce) container of yogurt, low-fat piña colada{1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix fruit in a large bowl.

Add yogurt and mix well.

Chill in refrigerator before serving.
