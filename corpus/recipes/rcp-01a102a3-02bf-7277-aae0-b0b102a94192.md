-- Ingredients

@ground turkey, 85% lean (or ground beef){1%pound}
@chopped onion{1%cup}
@taco seasoning packet{1}
@tomatoes and green chiles (10 ounce cans){2%cans}
@cooked rice (brown){3%cups}
@shredded cheese, low-fat{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a large skillet or pot, brown ground turkey and cook onion. When done, use paper towel to soak up any grease.

Add remaining ingredients except cheese and mix together.

Heat until very hot.

Top with cheese before serving.
