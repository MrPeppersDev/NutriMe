-- Ingredients

@olive oil or cooking oil{2%tablespoons}
@large red, yellow, or white onion (chopped){1}
@carrots (finely diced){3}
@garlic cloves (minced){3}
@lentils (1 pound, rinsed){2%cups}
@diced tomatoes (14 1/2 ounce can){1%can}
@low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth (2 quarts){8%cups}
@bay leaf{1}
@red pepper flakes{1/4%teaspoon}
@salt{1%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Sauté onions, carrots, red pepper flakes, and garlic in oil until soft.

Add tomatoes, bay leaf, salt, and pepper and stir well.

Add rinsed lentils and broth and simmer until lentils are soft, about 30 minutes. Keep checking lentils until they are done.

Season with additional salt and pepper, if desired.
