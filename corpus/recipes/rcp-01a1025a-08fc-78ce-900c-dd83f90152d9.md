>> title: Thai Coconut Chicken Curry (made in your slow cooker)
>> servings: 4
>> source: http://www.theendlessmeal.com/crock-pot-thai-chicken-curry/
>> attribution: theendlessmeal.com

-- Ingredients

@chicken stock (see notes){1-2%cups}
@Thai red curry paste (see notes){2-4%tablespoons}
@soy sauce{1%tablespoon}
@brown sugar{1%tablespoon}
@minced ginger{1%tablespoon}
@fish sauce (see notes){1%tablespoon}
@garlic (minced){3%cloves}
@boneless skinless chicken thighs (cut into bite sized pieces){1%lb}
@large kabocha squash (cut into 1-inch cubes - or 1 small butternut squash){1}
@medium yellow onion (chopped){1}
@chili peppers (if you like extra heat){1-2}
@can coconut milk{14%ounce}
@large bunch of kale (torn into small pieces, roughly 2 packed cups){1}
@Optional: cilantro, chili peppers, and lime to serve{}

-- Instructions

Place all the ingredients, except for the coconut milk and kale, into your crockpot and stir together well. Set your crockpot to cook on high for 4 hours.

After 4 hours, stir in the coconut milk and kale and let it sit while you prepare the rice, cauliflower rice, or quinoa to serve.

Top with a little cilantro, lime, and, if you like it extra spicy, some chopped chili peppers.
