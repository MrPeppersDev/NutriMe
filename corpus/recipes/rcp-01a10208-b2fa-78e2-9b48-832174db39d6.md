-- Ingredients

@Lettuce{1/2}
@Radish{7}
@Cucumber{1%sliced}
@Scallions{4%Chopped}
@Salt{To taste}
@Apple Cider Vinegar{2%tablespoons}
@Olive Oil{1/8%cup}
@Dill{Garnish}
@Parsley{Garnish}

-- Instructions

Place sliced lettuce in a large bowl.
Add radishes, cucumber, and scallions.
Drizzle with vinegar and olive oil.
Season with salt and gently toss to combine.
Top with sliced hard-boiled eggs, olives, dill and (or) parsley if using and serve immediately.
