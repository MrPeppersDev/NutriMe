-- Ingredients

@beef/pork/chicken/tofu (any cut, sliced thinly){1%lb}
@avocado oil{1%tbsp}
@brown sugar{1%tbsp}
@dark soy{1%tbsp}
@minced garlic{1%tbsp}
@minced lemongrass{1%tbsp}
@black pepper{1/2%tsp}
@onion powder{1/2%tsp}
@garlic{1/2%tsp}
@pack vermicelli noodles (cook to package){1}
@lettuce
@cucumber
@crushed peanuts{}
@Nước chấm sauce{}

-- Instructions

Mix all the beef and marinade ingredients together and set aside for at least 10 mins

Cook noodles and chop veggies

On med/high heat cook the beef for 5-7 mins or until its cooked

In a bowl: noodles, beef, veggies, peanuts and nuoc cham on the side (or on top!)
