-- Ingredients

@red potatoes or your favorite potato variety (about 1 pound){3}
@canola oil{1%tablespoon}
@red apples (Fuji, Pink Lady, Honeycrisp, Gala, etc.){2}
@yellow onion{1}
@chicken herb link sausages (12 ounces){4}
@cider vinegar{2%tablespoons}
@ground mustard{1/2%teaspoon}
@honey{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Preheat oven to 425 °F.

Cut potatoes into chunks, place in 2-quart baking dish.

Drizzle with canola oil. Toss to coat.

Roast potatoes in oven for about 20 minutes.

While potatoes are roasting, cut apples and onions into chunks, and sausage into 1/2-inch slices.

Remove baking dish from oven and reduce heat to 375 °F. Add all remaining ingredients to baking dish and toss.

Return baking dish to oven and roast an additional 30 minutes until apples and potatoes are tender.
