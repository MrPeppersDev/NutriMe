-- Ingredients

@low-fat mayonnaise{1/3%cup}
@nonfat or low-fat plain yogurt{1/3%cup}
@lemon juice{2%teaspoons}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@cooked chicken breast, chopped{3%cups}
@medium red apple, diced{1}
@red or green grapes, halved{1%cup}
@celery, sliced{1%cup}
@chopped walnuts, divided (toasted if desired){1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Whisk mayonnaise, yogurt, lemon juice and salt in a large bowl.

Add chicken, apple, grapes, celery, and half of the walnuts. Stir to coat well.

Serve topped with the remaining walnuts.
