>> title: Mango Blackberry Smoothie
>> servings: 2
>> source: http://lecremedelacrumb.com/2014/07/mango-blackberry-smoothie.html
>> attribution: lecremedelacrumb.com

-- Ingredients

@blackberries{2%cups}
@large mangoes (peeled and diced){2}
@crushed ice (OR 2 large ice cubes){1%cup}
@sugar ((or to taste)){2-4%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Place blackberries, half of the ice, and 1-2 tablespoons of sugar in your blender. Pulse until smooth. Pour into serving glasses.

Rinse out your blender. Add mangos, the rest of the ice, and the remaining 1-2 tablespoons of sugar to your blender. Pulse until smooth. Pour into serving glasses on top of blackberry mixture. Serve immediately.
