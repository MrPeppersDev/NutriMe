-- Ingredients

@(15.5 ounces) black beans, low-sodium (drained and rinsed){1%can}
@(14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes, low-sodium (drained){2%cans}
@green bell pepper (diced){1}
@red bell pepper (diced){1}
@onion (diced){1}
@garlic (diced){2%cloves}
@ground cumin{2%teaspoons}
@dried cilantro{1%teaspoon}
@rice vinegar (can use other vinegar such as red wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar){3%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Drain and rinse beans.

Put beans and canned tomatoes into large mixing bowl.

Dice peppers, onion, and garlic. Add to mixing bowl.

Mix cumin, cilantro, and vinegar. Pour on top of beans and vegetables.

Stir well.
