-- Ingredients

@Spinach{1%bunch}
@Tomato{2%medium}
@Onion{1%small}
@Oil{2%tablespoons}
@Curry Powder{2%tsp}
@Salt{To taste}
@Water{1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. When hot, add the diced onion and cook until lightly browned.
Add the curry powder and salt to taste and toast for a few seconds.
Add the chopped tomatoes and the water, stir well, and cook until the tomatoes begin to break down, 5-7 minutes.
Add in the chopped (or baby) spinach and cook for 7-10 minutes more, until soft and wilted.
