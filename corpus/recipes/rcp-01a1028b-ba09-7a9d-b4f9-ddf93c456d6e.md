-- Ingredients

@apples (such as Honeycrisp, cored and cut into 1” chunks and unpeeled){3}
@raisins{1/2%cup}
@lemon (juiced){1/2}
@old fashion oats{1/2%cup}
@whole wheat flour{1/4%cup}
@cinnamon{1%teaspoon}
@brown sugar{3%tablespoons}
@pistachios (unsalted, chopped){1/4%cup}
@margarine or butter (melted){2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Place rack in center of oven and preheat to 350 ºF.

Place sliced apples, raisins, and lemon juice in 8x8 inch pan or baking dish; toss.

In a bowl, mix remaining ingredients except melted margarine.

Add melted margarine and mix until texture is consistent. Sprinkle over apple-raisin mixture.

Bake uncovered 45-50 minutes or until apples are tender.
