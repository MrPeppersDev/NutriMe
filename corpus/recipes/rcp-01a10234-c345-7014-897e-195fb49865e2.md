-- Ingredients

@low-fat (1 percent) milk{2%C}
@frozen mango juice (or 1 fresh pitted mango){4%Tbsp}
@small banana{1}
@ice cubes{2}

-- Instructions

Put all ingredients into a blender. Blend until foamy. Serve immediately.
