-- Ingredients

@yogurt, non-fat vanilla{1/2%cup}
@blueberries or strawberries (fresh or frozen thawed){1%cup}
@low-fat granola (or crunchy cereal){1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Spoon yogurt into glass.

Top with fruit and granola.
