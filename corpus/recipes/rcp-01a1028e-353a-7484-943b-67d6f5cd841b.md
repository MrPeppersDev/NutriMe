-- Ingredients

@instant pearl barley{1%cup}
@water{4%cups}
@whole bay leaves{2}
@medium onions{3}
@small celery stalks{2}
@diced green, red, yellow, or orange bell pepper{1%cup}
@medium cloves garlic{2}
@canola oil{1%tablespoon}
@ground turkey{4%ounces}
@(14.5-oz) cans diced tomato (no salt added){2}
@salt{1%teaspoon}
@ground cayenne pepper{1/2%teaspoon}
@dried oregano{1 1/2%teaspoons}
@ground black pepper{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

In a colander, rinse barley under cold water.

In a medium pot over high heat, bring barley, water, and bay leaves to a boil. Reduce heat to low and cover. Cook barley until tender and water is absorbed, about 20-30 minutes.

In a colander, drain barley. Set aside.
