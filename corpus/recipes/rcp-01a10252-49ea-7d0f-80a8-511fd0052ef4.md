-- Ingredients

@TBS Neutral Oil ((such as vegetable or canola)){1}
@large Shallot (- finely diced){1}
@Ground Chicken ((SEE NOTES)){1%pound}
@Ginger (- freshly grated){1%tsp}
@Garlic (- minced){2%cloves}
@to taste Kosher Salt and Ground Black Pepper{}
@Unsalted Chicken Broth or Stock{1/2%Cup}
@Lemon Juice (-freshly squeezed (SEE NOTES)){1/4%Cup}
@whole Lemon (- ZEST only){1}
@Honey{1/3%Cup}
@TBS Reduced Sodium Soy Sauce{1}
@TBS Apple Cider Vinegar{1}
@TBS Cornstarch{1}
@Optional to taste Crushed Red Pepper Flakes ((substitute: Sriracha)){}
@Optional Additions: Shredded Zucchini, Shredded Squash, Microgreens, Sliced Radishes, Sliced Green Onions{}

-- Instructions

Make the Lemon Stir Fry Sauce: To a 2 cup glass measure or medium bowl, add the chicken broth, lemon juice, lemon zest, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, cornstarch, and red pepper flakes if using.Grab a whisk and vigorously stir the ingredients until they're fully combined. Then, set the sauce aside.

Stir-Fry Shallots, Then Ground Chicken, Add aromatics: Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, add the shallot and cook, stirring occasionally, for 1-2 minutes, or until just softened. Dump the ground chicken into the pan and season generously with salt and pepper. Stir fry, breaking up any lumps with a wooden spoon, until the chicken is almost golden brown, about 3-4 minutes.Add the garlic and ginger to the pan. Cook, stirring, until aromatic, about 30 seconds to 1 minute.

Add Lemon Sauce: Give the stir fry sauce a good whisk and then pour it over the chicken. Let the sauce come to a rapid simmer, then reduce the heat to maintain a simmer. Let the sauce simmer until slightly thickened, about 4 minutes. Remove the chicken stir fry from the heat and taste / adjust for seasoning as you see fit.

Serve Lemon Chicken Stir Fry: Serve the lemony chicken stir fry over rice or noodles and garnish with toasted sesame seeds and chives or green onions. Enjoy immediately.
