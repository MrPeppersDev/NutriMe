-- Ingredients

@honey*{2%tablespoons}
@water{1/2%cup}
@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){2%tablespoons}
@vanilla extract{1%teaspoon}
@cinnamon{1%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@uncooked rolled oats (oatmeal){4%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 275 °F.

Mix honey, water, vegetable oil, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl.

Stir in oats; mix well.

Spread mixture on a cookie sheet, making a thin layer.

Bake for 20 minutes. Remove from oven, stir mixture well and return to oven.

Bake 20 minutes more and stir as before.

Bake 5 to 20 minutes more, until granola is golden brown.

Break into small pieces with spatula.

Cool and then store in a covered container.
