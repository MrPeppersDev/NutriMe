-- Ingredients

@all-purpose flour (or 1 1/2 cup all purpose flour and 1/2 cup whole wheat flour){2%cups}
@baking powder{1%teaspoon}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@vegetable oil{2%tablespoons}
@water (warm, 105 to 110 °F){1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, and salt.

Add oil and mix until crumbly.

Start with 1/2 cup of water and add 1 tablespoon at a time until mixture forms a loose crumbly ball.

Turn onto a lightly floured surface; knead 6 to 8 times or until smooth.

Divide into 10 balls. Roll each piece into an 8 inch circle.

Heat a 12 inch dry skillet over medium-high heat. Place one tortilla at a time into skillet and cook until light brown spots appear on bottom. Turn over and cook on other side until lightly browned.
