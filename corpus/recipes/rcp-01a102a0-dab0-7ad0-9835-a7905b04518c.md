-- Ingredients

@large sweet potatoes{2}
@non-fat yogurt{2%tablespoons}
@maple syrup{1%tablespoon}
@orange juice (1 tablespoon){1/16%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Prick potato skins with a fork. Microwave on high for 3 to 4 minute until soft and easily pierced with a knife.

Scoop out the pulp into a medium bowl.

Mash the pulp and stir in the yogurt, maple syrup, and orange juice.

Transfer to a microwave safe serving bowl and microwave for 1 to 2 minutes to heat through.
