-- Ingredients

@lentils (dry){1%cup}
@water{5%cups}
@vegetable oil{2%tablespoons}
@onion (finely chopped){1%tablespoon}
@canned tomato sauce, low-sodium{1%cup}
@garlic powder (or 1 garlic clove){1/2%teaspoon}
@salt{1%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@bay leaf{1}
@celery stalk (chopped){1}
@vinegar{2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Sort lentils.

Place lentils in deep pot with water. Bring to a boil and add all ingredients except vinegar.

Reduce heat and cover.

Cook slowly for 1 hour, adding more water if necessary.

Add vinegar.

Remove garlic and bay leaf before serving.
