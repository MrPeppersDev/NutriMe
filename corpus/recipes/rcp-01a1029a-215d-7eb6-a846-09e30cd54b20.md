-- Ingredients

@banana, medium, very ripe, (peeled){1}
@pineapple juice{3/4%cup}
@yogurt, low-fat vanilla{1/2%cup}
@strawberries (remove stems and rinse){1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Blend banana with pineapple juice, yogurt, and strawberries in a blender.

Blend until smooth.

Divide shake between 2 glasses and serve immediately.
