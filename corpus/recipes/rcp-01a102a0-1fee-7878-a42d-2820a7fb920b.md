-- Ingredients

@garlic powder (or 1 fresh clove, minced){1/2%teaspoon}
@Italian herb mix{1%teaspoon}
@Parmesan cheese{1%tablespoon}
@black pepper (ground){1/4%teaspoon}
@celery salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@vinegar{1/2%cup}
@vegetable oil{1/4%cup}
@water{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a pint sized jar with tight fitting lid.

Shake vigorously to blend ingredients.

Store refrigerated.
