-- Ingredients

@skinless cooked chicken (diced into 1/2 inch pieces){1%cup}
@15.5 ounce low-sodium white beans (drained and rinsed with cold water){1%can}
@cucumber (peeled and diced into 1/2 inch pieces){1}
@red or white onion (peeled and chopped into 1/2 inch pieces){1/4}
@vegetable oil{2%tablespoons}
@lemon juice{1/4%cup}
@dried basil or parsley leaves{1%tablespoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Put everything in the bowl and gently toss.

Serve right away, or cover and refrigerate for up to 2 days.
