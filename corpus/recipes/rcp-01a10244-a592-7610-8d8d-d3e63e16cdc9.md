>> title: Savory Mille-Feuille with Lemon Ricotta & Prosciutto
>> servings: 3
>> source: https://crisptastes.com/savory-mille-feuille-with-lemon-ricotta-prosciutto/?tp_image_id=14010&pin_title=U2F2b3J5IE1pbGxlLUZldWlsbGUgd2l0aCBMZW1vbiBSaWNvdHRhICYgUHJvc2NpdXR0bw%3D%3D
>> attribution: crisptastes.com

-- Ingredients

@sheet all-butter puff pastry, thawed{1}
@egg (for egg wash){1}
@Flaky sea salt (for topping){}
@ricotta cheese{250%g}
@Zest of 1 lemon{}
@lemon juice{1%tbsp}
@olive oil{1%tbsp}
@Salt & pepper to taste{}
@prosciutto, very thinly sliced{100%g}
@Fresh thyme leaves or microgreens (optional, for garnish){}

-- Instructions

Preheat oven to 200°C (390°F). Cut puff pastry into 6 rectangles (about 10×5 cm). Place them on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Prick with a fork, brush with egg wash, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt. Cover with a second parchment sheet and another tray. Bake for 15–18 minutes until golden and crisp. Let cool completely.

id=”instruction-step-2″>2. Whisk ricotta with lemon zest, juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper until smooth and airy. Transfer to a piping bag for cleaner assembly, if desired.

id=”instruction-step-3″>3. Place one pastry rectangle on a plate. Pipe or spoon whipped ricotta on top. Add a few folds of prosciutto. Repeat with another pastry, ricotta, and prosciutto. Finish with a final pastry layer.

id=”instruction-step-4″>4. Garnish with thyme leaves or microgreens. Serve immediately for best texture.
