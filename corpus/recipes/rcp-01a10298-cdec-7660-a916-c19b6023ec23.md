-- Ingredients

@fresh corn, cooked (or frozen){1/2%cup}
@(15 ounces) low-sodium black beans, drained and rinsed{1%can}
@tomatoes, diced{1%cup}
@onions, diced (yellow or red){1/2%cup}
@green pepper, diced{1/2%cup}
@lime juice{2%tablespoons}
@garlic clove, finely chopped{2}
@picante sauce{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a large bowl. Chill until serving time.

Drain before serving.

Serve with low-fat baked tortilla chips or fresh vegetables.
