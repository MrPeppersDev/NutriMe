-- Ingredients

@whole wheat flour tortillas, 10" across{3}
@sprays of cooking oil spray{3}
@salt (dash, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven or toaster oven to 400 °F.

Lightly grease a baking sheet with the cooking spray.

Cut tortillas into 8 sections (as if you were cutting a pizza) and place on baking sheet.

Spray tops of tortilla sections with cooking spray and lightly salt, if desired.

Bake for 10 minutes, until crisp and light brown, watching closely so the chips don't burn.
