-- Ingredients

@coconut cream from refrigerated canned coconut milk or coconut cream*{1/2%cup}
@raw and unsalted pistachios (soak them in hot water for 30 minutes so they are soft and blendable ){1/2%cup}
@coconut sugar{1/4%cup}
@block silken tofu - drained{1}
@vanilla extract{1/2%teaspoon}
@pinch salt{}

-- Instructions

Add all ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth.

Divide out pudding into jars, glasses or your favorite vessel. Refrigerate for 30 minutes - 1 hour.

Top pudding with your favorite toppings and enjoy.
