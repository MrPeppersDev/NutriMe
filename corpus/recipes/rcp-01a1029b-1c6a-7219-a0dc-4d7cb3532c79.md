-- Ingredients

@ground beef, 90% lean{1%pound}
@large onion (chopped){1}
@brown sugar{1/4%cup}
@ketchup{1/2%cup}
@vinegar{2%tablespoons}
@black pepper{1/2%teaspoon}
@kidney beans (cooked, or 15 ounce can, low-sodium){2%cups}
@pork and beans (16 ounce){1%can}
@great northern beans (cooked, or a 15 ounce can great northern beans, low-sodium){2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Cook ground beef and onions. Drain fat.

Add remaining ingredients and mix.

Place in casserole dish.

Bake in the oven at 350 °F for 1 hour.
