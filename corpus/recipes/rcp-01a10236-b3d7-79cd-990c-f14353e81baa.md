-- Ingredients

@(15.5 ounces) beans, low-sodium, undrained (pinto, kidney, red, or black){1%can}
@(15.2 ounces) corn, drained (or 10-oz package of frozen corn){1%can}
@(14.5 ounces) crushed tomatoes, low-sodium, undrained{1%can}
@chili powder (or more, to taste){1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place the contents of all 3 cans into a pan.

Add chili powder to taste.

Stir to mix.

Continue to stir over medium heat until heated thoroughly.

Refrigerate leftovers.
