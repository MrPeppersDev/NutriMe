-- Ingredients

@medium head of lettuce (about 10 cups){1}
@medium apples, sliced{2}
@chopped walnuts{1/2%cup}
@dried cranberries{1%cup}
@green onions, sliced{1/2%cup}
@vinaigrette dressing{3/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toss lettuce, apples, walnuts, cranberries, and onions in a large bowl.

Add dressing; toss to coat. Serve immediately.
