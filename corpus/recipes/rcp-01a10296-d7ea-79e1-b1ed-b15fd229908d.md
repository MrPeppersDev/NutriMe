-- Ingredients

@cucumber, diced{2%cups}
@tomato, seeded and diced{1%cup}
@sweet onion, chopped{1/4%cup}
@couscous, cooked (or rice){2%cups}
@dill weed, chopped{2%teaspoons}
@Italian salad dressing, low-fat{1/2%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toss together the cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, couscous (or rice), dill, and salad dressing.

Chill for 1 hour.

Serve.
