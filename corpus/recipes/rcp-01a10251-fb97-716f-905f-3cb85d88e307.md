>> title: Spicy Thai Lemongrass Ginger Margarita
>> servings: 2
>> source: http://boulderlocavore.com/spicy-thai-lemongrass-ginger-margarita-recipe-and-food-stories-workshop/comment-page-1/#comment-301006
>> attribution: boulderlocavore.com

-- Ingredients

@1-inch long fresh Thai Bird Chile{1}
@Gourmet Garden Lemongrass Stir-In Paste{1%tablespoon}
@Gourmet Garden Ginger Stir-In Paste{1 1/2%teaspoons}
@freshly squeezed Lime Juice{1 1/2%ounces}
@Blanco (White or Silver) Tequila{3%ounces}
@Agave Nectar{1%ounce}
@Optional garnish: lime wedges{}

-- Instructions

In a shaker or quart Mason jar, combine the chile, lemongrass, ginger and lime juice. Muddle the chile well.

Add the tequila, agave nectar and 1 1/2 cups of ice. Shake vigorously for 1 minute.

Strain into 2 8-ounce Old Fashioned glass filled with fresh ice. Garnish with lime wedge (optional).
