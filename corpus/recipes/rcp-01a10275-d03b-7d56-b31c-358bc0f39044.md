>> attribution: pickledplum.com

-- Ingredients

@carrots, peeled and chopped{7%ounces}
@onion, peeled and finely chopped{4%ounces}
@ginger, peeled and finely chopped{2%tablespoons}
@granulated sugar{1%tablespoon}
@soy sauce{1/4%cup}
@rice vinegar{1/2%cup}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@organic canola or vegetable oil{3/4%cup}

-- Instructions

. Put all the ingredients except for the oil in a blender and blend until smooth.

. Slowly add the oil and blend until the mixture has emulsified.

. Use this dressing with a crisp lettuce like iceberg or romaine.
