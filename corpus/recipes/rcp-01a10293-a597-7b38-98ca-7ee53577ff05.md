-- Ingredients

@garlic cloves{3}
@yogurt, plain, fat-free{1/4%cup}
@lemon juice, fresh{1%tablespoon}
@olive oil (or cooking oil of choice){1%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@paprika{1/4%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/8%teaspoon}
@(15 ounces) chickpeas, low-sodium, drained (garbanzo beans){1%can}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Put all ingredients into a food processor and blend until smooth.

Serve at room temperature with pita chips, crackers, carrots, or other dipping vegetables.
