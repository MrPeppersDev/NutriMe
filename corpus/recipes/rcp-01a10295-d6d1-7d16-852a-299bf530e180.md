-- Ingredients

@mixed nuts (1/2 cup each almonds, walnuts, pistachios and hazelnuts){2%cups}
@seeds (1/3 cup each pumpkin seeds and sunflower seeds){2/3%cup}
@unsweetened dried fruit (1/4 cup each dried cranberries, cherries, blueberries and goji berries){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Add all of the ingredients to a large mixing bowl.

Gently toss the ingredients to mix things up.

Store in an air-tight container for up to 30 days.
