-- Ingredients

@sweet potatoes (small to medium){4}
@thyme (fresh or dried){3/4%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash and peel the potatoes. Cut them into slices that are 3/4 inch thick.

Put them in a saucepan with enough water to cover the potatoes.

Bring the water to a boil on medium heat.

Cook the potatoes for 20 to 25 minutes until they’re soft.

Drain the water. Put the potatoes in a medium bowl.

Use a fork or potato masher to mash the potatoes.

Mix in the thyme, salt, and pepper.
