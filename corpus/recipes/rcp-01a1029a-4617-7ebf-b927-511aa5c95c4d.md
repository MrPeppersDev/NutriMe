-- Ingredients

@yogurt, low-fat vanilla (8 oz){1%cup}
@us fluid ounce orange juice, 100% juice, frozen concentrate (thawed){1}
@lime juice{1%tablespoon}
@brown sugar (1/2 tablespoon){1 1/2%teaspoons}
@red apples (cored and sliced){2}
@pear (cored and sliced){1}
@peach (pitted and sliced){1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a small bowl, combine yogurt, orange juice concentrate, lime juice, and brown sugar.

Mix well.

Place bowl containing dip on a large plate and surround with fruit.
