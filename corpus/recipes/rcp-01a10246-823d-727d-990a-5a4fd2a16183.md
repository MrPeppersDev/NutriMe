-- Ingredients

@angel hair cabbage{10%ounces}
@mayonnaise{3%tablespoons}
@soy sauce{2%tablespoons}
@rice vinegar{1%tablespoon}
@toasted sesame oil{1%teaspoon}
@white sesame seeds{}

-- Instructions

Combine 3 tablespoons of mayonnaise, 2 tablespoons of soy, 1 tablespoon of rice vinegar and 1 teaspoon of toasted sesame oil in a large bowl

Add 10 ounces of angel hair cabbage and toss until all of the cabbage is lightly coated

Garnish with white sesame seeds

Enjoy immediately
