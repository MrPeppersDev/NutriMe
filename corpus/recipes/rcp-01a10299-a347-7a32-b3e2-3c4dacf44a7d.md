-- Ingredients

@medium tomatoes, chopped{2}
@onion, chopped{1/2}
@jalapeno chiles, finely chopped, seeded if desired{3}
@cilantro, chopped{1/4%cup}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@lime, juiced{1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a medium bowl, mix all ingredients.

Serve or store salsa in refrigerator for up to three days in a covered plastic or glass container.
