>> notes: To avoid broken or mushy beans, boil gently and stir very little. Taste-test beans often for desired tenderness. Foods containing acid such as tomatoes, chili sauce, lemon juice, vinegar, or catsup will slow the cooking and softening of the beans. Add these items last so they will not add to the cooking time. Any of the following can be added during the last half hour of cooking: minced onion, garlic or green pepper, diced carrots or celery, chopped tomatoes, or cooked meat.

-- Ingredients

@dried beans{1%cup}
@water{10%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Sort : Before soaking beans, pick them over and remove any damaged beans, small stones or dirt.

Soak : Most beans will rehydrate to triple their dry size, so be sure to start with a large enough pot. Choose one of the methods to soak your beans.

Cook : Drain soaking water and rinse beans. Cover beans with fresh water. Simmer for 1 1/2 to 2 hours until tender.
