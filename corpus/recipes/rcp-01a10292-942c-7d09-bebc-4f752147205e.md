-- Ingredients

@onion, chopped{1%cup}
@green pepper, chopped{1/4%cup}
@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){2%teaspoons}
@(8 ounces) tomato sauce, low sodium{1%can}
@parsley, chopped (1 teaspoon){1/3%tablespoon}
@black pepper{1/2%teaspoon}
@garlic, minced{1 1/4%teaspoons}
@cooked brown rice (cook rice in unsalted water){5%cups}
@cooked chicken breast, diced, skin and bone removed{3 1/2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a large skillet, sauté onions and green peppers in oil for 5 minutes on medium heat.

Add tomato sauce and spices. Heat through.

Add cooked rice and chicken. Heat through.
