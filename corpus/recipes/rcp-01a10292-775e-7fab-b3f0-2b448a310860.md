-- Ingredients

@chicken, cooked and diced (1 1/2 cups){12%ounces}
@vinaigrette dressing (about 1/2 cup){1}
@dried cranberries (or cherries){1%cup}
@almonds, sliced{1/8%cup}
@lettuce, chopped{1%head}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toss chicken, cranberries, and almonds with dressing.

Serve on a mound of chopped lettuce.
