-- Ingredients

@seedless grapes (cut up){2%cups}
@green onions (sliced){1/2%cup}
@Anaheim chili, fresh or canned (diced){1/2%cup}
@cilantro (chopped){2%tablespoons}
@vinegar{2%tablespoons}
@garlic (minced or 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder){1%clove}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@hot sauce{1/8%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl and mix well. Let stand at least 1 hour. Drain off excess liquid before serving.
