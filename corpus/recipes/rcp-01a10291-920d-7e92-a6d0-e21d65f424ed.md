-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){1%tablespoon}
@chopped onion{1%cup}
@medium green peppers, chopped{2}
@small head cabbage, chopped{1}
@garlic, chopped (or 1/2 teaspoon of garlic powder){2%cloves}
@soy sauce, low-sodium{2%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash green peppers and cabbage before chopping.

Heat oil in a large skillet.

Add onions, peppers, cabbage, and garlic to skillet and cook over medium heat until vegetables are tender.

Add soy sauce and stir until sauce boils.

Refrigerate leftovers.
