-- Ingredients

@olive oil{2%tbsp}
@lemon juice{2%tbsp}
@garlic cloves{2}
@Garam Masala{2%tsp}
@curry powder{1%tsp}
@salt{1%tsp}
@black pepper{1/2%tsp}
@(1 1/2lb) fish fillets{800%g}
@Basmati rice (Jasmine rice is a great alternative){2%cups}
@boiling water{4%cups}
@salt{1%tsp}
@ground turmeric{1%tsp}

-- Instructions

To make the rice, combine the rice, boiling water, salt and turmeric in a saucepan set over medium-high heat. Bring to a simmer then cover partially with a lid and allow to cook for 8-10 minutes until the water has been absorbed. Cover fully, reduce the heat to low and allow to cook for another 5 minutes then switch off the heat and allow to steam for another 10 minutes.

Preheat the oven to 200°C/390°F.

Combine olive oil, a generous squeeze of lemon juice, minced garlic, Garlic Masala, curry powder, salt and pepper together in a small bowl.

Line a baking sheet or baking dish with parchment paper.

Add the fish, skin-side down.

Pat the fish dry with paper towels then spoon over the masala mixture, spreading to coat the fish completely.

Place the fish in the oven and allow to bake for 10-12 minutes until the fish is cooked through.

Remove from the oven and allow to rest for a few minutes then serve with side dishes of your choice.
