-- Ingredients

@ground beef (lean){1%pound}
@onion (medium, chopped){1}
@water{3 1/2%cups}
@tomato sauce, low-sodium (15 ounces){1%can}
@dried oregano{2%teaspoons}
@sugar{1/2%teaspoon}
@garlic powder{1/2%teaspoon}
@rosemary{1/2%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@spaghetti noodles (broken){16%ounces}
@Parmesan cheese (shredded){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Brown meat and onions in a large skillet over medium-high heat (300 °F in an electric skillet). Drain fat.

Stir in water, tomato sauce, and spices; bring to a boil.

Add spaghetti, cover pan, and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often to prevent sticking.

When spaghetti is tender, top with grated cheese.

Refrigerate leftovers with in 2 hours.
