-- Ingredients

@water{3%cups}
@single-serving bags of green tea{8}
@frozen mixed berries (~16 ounces){3%cups}
@medium bananas, peeled, cut in half and frozen{2}
@fat-free plain Greek yogurt{1%cup}
@fresh grated peeled gingerroot (optional){2%teaspoons}
@vanilla extract (optional){2%teaspoons}
@chia or flax seeds (optional){2%teaspoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Add the water to a small saucepan. Bring the water to a boil over high heat. Remove the pan from the heat.

Add the tea bags. (Be sure the tea bags are submerged.) Let steep for 4 minutes. Discard the tea bags.

Let the tea mixture cool for 30 minutes. Pour into a water pitcher. Refrigerate for 2 hours, or until chilled.

In a food processor or blender, process the tea, berries, bananas, yogurt, gingerroot and vanilla until smooth.

Pour into four glasses. Sprinkle with the chia seeds.
