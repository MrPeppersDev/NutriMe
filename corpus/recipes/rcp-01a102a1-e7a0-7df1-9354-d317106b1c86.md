-- Ingredients

@10-ounce packages frozen vegetables, any type{1}
@stewed tomatoes, canned, low-sodium (14.5 ounce){2%cans}
@broth, any flavor, canned, low-sodium (14 ounce cans){2%cans}
@beans, canned, any type, low-sodium (15.5 ounce){1%can}
@pasta, dry, any type (1 cup){1%ounce}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Adult and child : Wash hands well with soap and hot water.

Child : In a large pot, combine frozen vegetables, tomatoes, broth and beans.

Adult : Bring the soup to a boil and add the pasta. Then reduce to low heat. Let simmer for 6 to 8 minutes or until the pasta and vegetables are tender.
