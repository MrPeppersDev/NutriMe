-- Ingredients

@garlic clove{1}
@packed cup chopped cilantro{1}
@avocado{1}
@juice of 1 lime(about 4 tbsp) plus more to taste{}
@cooked and cooled chickpeas (or one 15 oz can drained and rinsed){1 1/2%cup}
@medium avocado{1}
@olive oil{1-2%tbsp}
@salt plus more to taste{1/4%tsp}
@cayenne pepper to taste{}

-- Instructions

Add the garlic and cilantro to the food processor and pulse until the garlic is in tiny pieces. Add the chickpeas and process, scraping down the sides until combined. Add the avocado, lime juice, salt, cayenne and olive oil and process until smooth. Taste and add more lime juice, salt or olive oil as needed.
