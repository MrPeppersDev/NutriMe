-- Ingredients

@us fluid ounces orange juice{16}
@water (cold){2%cups}
@milk (non-fat, dry){1/3%cup}
@vanilla{1%teaspoon}
@ice (or more){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place all ingredients in blender or food processor.

Secure lid and blend until smooth. Serve immediately.
