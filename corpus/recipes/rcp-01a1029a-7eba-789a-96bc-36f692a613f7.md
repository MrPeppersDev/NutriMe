-- Ingredients

@milk, fat-free (or water){1%cup}
@non-fat dry milk{1/2%cup}
@ripe bananas (or 1/3 cup orange juice concentrate){2}
@vanilla{1/2%teaspoon}
@large ice cubes{10}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place everything together into a blender.

Blend for 30 seconds to 1 minute.
