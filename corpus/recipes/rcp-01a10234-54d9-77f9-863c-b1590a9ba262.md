>> title: Cold Fusilli Pasta with Summer Vegetables
>> servings: 4
>> source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living/healthy-foods/healthy-eating-recipes/cold-fusilli-pasta-summer
>> attribution: NHLBI Delicious Heart Healthy Eating

-- Ingredients

@whole-wheat fusilli (spiral) pasta{8%oz}
@cherry tomatoes, rinsed and halved{2%C}
@large green bell pepper, rinsed and sliced in pieces 1/4 inch wide by 2 inches long{1}
@red onion, thinly sliced{1/2%C}
@medium zucchini, rinsed and shredded finely or sliced into small chunks (about 1 C){1}
@(15 1/2 oz) low-sodium chickpeas (or garbanzo beans), drained and rinsed{1%can}
@fresh basil, rinsed, dried, and cut into thin strips (or 1 tsp dried){1%Tbsp}
@salt{1/4%tsp}
@ground black pepper{1/8%tsp}
@extra virgin olive oil{1%Tbsp}
@balsamic vinegar{2%Tbsp}
@shredded parmesan cheese{1/2%C}

-- Instructions

In a 4-quart saucepan, bring 3 quarts of water to a boil over high heat.

Add pasta, and cook according to package directions for the shortest recommended time, about 8–9 minutes. Drain. Rinse pasta under cold running water to cool, about 3 minutes.

Place all the vegetables and beans in a large salad serving bowl. Season with basil, salt, and pepper.

Add the cooled pasta.

Combine olive oil and vinegar in a small bowl. Mix until completely blended. Pour over vegetables and pasta. Mix gently until well coated.

Divide into four equal portions. Top each with 2 tablespoons shredded parmesan cheese.
