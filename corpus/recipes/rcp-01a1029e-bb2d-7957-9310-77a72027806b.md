-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice), divided{2%tablespoons}
@medium summer squash, thinly sliced{1}
@water{1%tablespoon}
@salt and pepper (optional, to taste){}
@fresh basil{1%tablespoon}
@Parmesan cheese or Romano cheese, grated{6%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Using a large, oven-proof frying pan, heat 1 tablespoon of oil on medium-high.

Arrange squash in pan, add water, and season lightly with salt, pepper, if using, and basil.

Cover and cook over medium heat for 5 minutes, or until tender crisp.

Sprinkle with cheese and drizzle with remaining oil. Place under a preheated broiler and broil until cheese melts and browns slightly.
