-- Ingredients

@corn tortillas (6 inch){4}
@vegetable oil{1/2%tablespoon}
@non-stick cooking spray{}
@egg whites (medium){4}
@eggs (medium){4}
@black pepper (ground){1/8%teaspoon}
@cheese, shredded cheddar or monterey jack{4%tablespoons}
@fresh salsa{2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 450 °F.

Wash hands with soap and water.

Lightly brush tortillas with oil on both sides and place on a baking sheet.

Bake for 5 to 10 minutes or until tortillas are crisp on the edges and starting to brown.

Remove from oven and set aside.

Spray a large skillet with non-stick cooking spray over medium heat.

Drop 4 egg whites into skillet, then, break whole eggs over whites to make 4 separate servings.

Cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side until eggs are cooked.

Place one egg on each tortilla shell and top each with 1 Tablespoon cheese

Place under the broiler for about 2 minutes until cheese is melted.

Spoon 1/2 cup fresh salsa around the edge of each shell.
