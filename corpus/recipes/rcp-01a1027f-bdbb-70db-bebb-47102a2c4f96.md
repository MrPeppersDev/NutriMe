>> servings: 4

>> attribution: fearlessdining.com

-- Ingredients

@chia seeds (* see note){3%tablespoons}
@matcha green tea powder{1%tablespoon}
@coconut milk (* see note){1 1/3%cups}
@unsweetened coconut{1/2%cup}
@cane sugar (or less if you don't like it sweet!){1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

In a large glass container, add 3 tablespoons chia seeds, 1 tablespoon matcha green tea powder, 1 1/3 cups coconut milk, 1/2 cup unsweetened coconut, and 1 tablespoon cane sugar.

Put on lid, shake well, and store in refrigerator overnight.

Stir after the first hour, or before you go to bed.

Stir again before serving.

This recipe is also delicious when you garnish it with sliced almonds and more coconut.
