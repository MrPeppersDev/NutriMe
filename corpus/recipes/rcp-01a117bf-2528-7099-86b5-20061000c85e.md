-- Ingredients

@pecans{2%cups}
@maple syrup{1/4%cup}
@vanilla{1%tsp}
@cinnamon{1/2%tsp}
@Pinch of salt{}

-- Instructions

Toss pecans with maple, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt.

Spread on baking sheet.

Roast at 325° F for 20 minutes.
