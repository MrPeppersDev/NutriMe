-- Ingredients

@pineapple, canned and chopped{2%cups}
@frozen berries, unsweetened and thawed{1%cup}
@yogurt, low-fat vanilla{1%cup}
@banana, peeled and sliced{1}
@raisins{1/3%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In glasses or bowls, layer pineapple, berries, yogurt, banana, and raisins.
