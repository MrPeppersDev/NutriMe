-- Ingredients

@margarine or butter{4%tablespoons}
@celery (diced){2%cups}
@onion (chopped){1%cup}
@whole wheat bread, cubed{8%slices}
@parsley (chopped fresh or dried){2%tablespoons}
@mushroom (sliced){1/4%cup}
@sage (ground){1%teaspoon}
@poultry seasoning{3/4%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1%teaspoon}
@broth, chicken or turkey, low-sodium{3%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toast bread cubes and set aside for later use.

Melt margarine or butter in medium size fry pan.

Add celery and onion; cook until tender, about 10 minutes.

Add parsley, mushrooms, seasoning, and broth. Cook for 5 minutes.

Remove from heat and add the toasted bread cubes. Cover and let stand 10 minutes. Serve immediately.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
