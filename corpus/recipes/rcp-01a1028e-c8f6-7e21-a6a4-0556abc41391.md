-- Ingredients

@fresh mango cubes{2%cups}
@unflavored gelatin sheets (15g){3}
@granulated sugar ( , or more if needed){1/2%cup}
@water{1%cup}
@cream{100%ml}
@milk{150%ml}

-- Instructions

Soak gelatin sheets in cold water until soft.

In a small pot, dissolve sugar in clean water. Then heat the water temperature among 50℃ to 70℃, place gelatin in. Stir until dissolved.

Cut fresh mango in half and then dice the fresh mangos out. Blend well with the gleatine liquid. Blend well.

Add milk, cream and mix well.

Divide into four containers. Chill until firmed.

Before eating, decorate the fresh mango dices.
