>> title: Strawberry Banana Yogurt Parfait
>> servings: 8
>> source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living/healthy-foods/healthy-eating-recipes/strawberry-banana-yogurt-parfait
>> attribution: NHLBI Delicious Heart Healthy Eating

-- Ingredients

@fat-free, no-sugar-added vanilla yogurt{4%C}
@large bananas, sliced (about 2 C){2}
@fresh strawberries, sliced (or use thawed frozen fruit){2%C}
@crumbled graham crackers{2%C}
@fat-free whipped topping (optional){1/2%C}
@8-oz wine or parfait glasses{4}

-- Instructions

Spoon 1 tablespoon of yogurt into the bottom of each 8-ounce wine or parfait glass. Top the yogurt with 1 tablespoon sliced bananas, 1 tablespoon sliced strawberries, and ¼ cup graham cracker crumbs.

Repeat the yogurt, banana, strawberry, and graham cracker layers.

Top with a rounded tablespoon of fat-free whipped topping, if desired.

Serve the parfait immediately, or cover each glass with plastic wrap and chill for up to 2 hours before serving.
