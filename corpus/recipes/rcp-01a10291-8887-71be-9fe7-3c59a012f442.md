-- Ingredients

@cabbage (shredded or thinly sliced){4%cups}
@carrot (grated or finely sliced){1/4%cup}
@celery (thinly sliced){1/4%cup}
@green pepper (chopped){1/4%cup}
@onion (finely chopped){1%teaspoon}
@dried dill (may also use dried basil or parsley){1/2%teaspoon}
@mayonnaise, light or low-fat{1/2%cup}
@plain yogurt, non-fat{1/2%cup}
@mustard{1%teaspoon}
@lemon juice{1/2%teaspoon}
@sugar{1/2%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash and prepare vegetables for chopping.

Use a cheese grater or slice all vegetables thinly with a sharp knife.

Put in a large bowl.

Add dill and/or other herbs to taste.

Mix mayonnaise, yogurt, mustard, lemon juice, and sugar in a small bowl. Blend well. Add to vegetables.

Mix well. Keep in refrigerator until ready to eat.
