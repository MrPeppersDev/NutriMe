-- Ingredients

@ground beef, 90% lean{3/4%pound}
@water{1 1/2%cups}
@macaroni, uncooked{1%cup}
@(14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes, low-sodium, undrained{1%can}
@chili powder, mild{2%teaspoons}
@servings Eating Smart Seasoning Mix (1/2 cup){2}
@salt (optional, to taste){}
@cheddar cheese, reduced-fat, shredded{2%ounces}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Brown ground beef in a large skillet, drain the fat.

Add water, macaroni, tomatoes, chili powder, and seasoning mix. Stir.

Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer covered on low heat for 20 minutes or until macaroni is tender.

Taste, and add a small amount of salt if desired.

Top with shredded cheddar cheese. Put the lid back on for 1 minute until the cheese is melted.
