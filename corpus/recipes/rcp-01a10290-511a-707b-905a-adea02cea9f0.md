-- Ingredients

@bread, cubed (approximately 6 cups){6%slices}
@broccoli, frozen, chopped and cooked{1 1/2%cups}
@cheddar cheese, low-fat shredded{1%cup}
@onion, minced (optional, 1 tablespoon){}
@cooked chicken, skinless and diced{1%cup}
@eggs{3}
@egg whites{4}
@milk, non-fat{2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place half the bread in a well greased 9x9 inch pan.

Top with broccoli, cheese, onion, and meat.

Place remaining bread on top.

In a bowl, mix eggs and milk.

Pour egg mixture over bread in pan.

Cover. Refrigerate overnight or at least 1 hour.

Bake uncovered at 325 °F for 1 to 1 1/4 hours, or until center is firm and lightly browned.
