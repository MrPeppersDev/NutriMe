-- Ingredients

@yogurt, low-fat vanilla{1/2%cup}
@peanuts (chopped){2%cups}
@fruit (grapes, banana slices, apple wedges, strawberries){2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine yogurt and peanuts. Mix with a spoon.

Place a toothpick in the center of each fruit.

Dip fruit into the yogurt peanut mixture.
