-- Ingredients

@apples{3}
@sugar{3%tablespoons}
@water{1/4%cup}
@eggs{2}
@low-fat or fat-free milk{1 1/2%cups}
@honey{1%tablespoon}
@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){3%tablespoons}
@whole wheat flour{3/4%cup}
@flour (all purpose){3/4%cup}
@baking powder{2%teaspoons}
@bananas{2}
@cinnamon{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Wash apples, remove cores, and slice thinly with peel still on.

Combine apples with the sugar, cinnamon, and water.

Cook in skillet for 10 minutes. Set aside while making pancakes.
