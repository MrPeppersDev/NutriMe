>> title: Lentil Barley Soup
>> servings: 6
>> source: https://www.myplate.gov/recipes/lentil-barley-soup
>> attribution: USDA MyPlate Kitchen
>> description: A big pot of lentils, tomatoes, and pearl barley simmer into this hearty soup. A great "make ahead" for quick meals and easy clean up.
>> calories_per_serving: 155.00
>> notes: Learn more about: Onions Carrots Herbs Tomatoes

-- Ingredients

@dried lentils (rinsed){2%cups}
@scallions{4}
@carrots (chopped){5}
@dried oregano{2%teaspoons}
@pearl barley{1/2%cup}
@water{12%cups}
@14.5 ounce low-sodium whole peeled tomatoes (coarsely chopped){1%can}
@red wine vinegar{1%tablespoon}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Place a large soup pot on the stove and add lentils, scallions, carrots, celery, oregano, barley, and water. Bring to a boil.

Reduce the heat to low and simmer, uncovered, for one hour.

Add the tomatoes and continue cooking about 45 minutes. Just before serving, add vinegar.
