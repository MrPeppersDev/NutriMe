-- Ingredients

@(20 ounces) pineapple chunks in juice, drained{1%can}
@canned mandarin orange, drained{1 1/3%cups}
@banana, peeled and sliced{1}
@seedless grapes{1 1/2%cups}
@miniature marshmallows{3/4%cup}
@flaked coconut{1/3%cup}
@vanilla yogurt, low-fat (about 8 ounces){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Drain pineapple and oranges. Use juice as beverage.

Combine fruit with marshmallows and coconut.

Fold in yogurt.

Chill.

Serve.
