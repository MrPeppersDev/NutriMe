-- Ingredients

@large cucumbers (peeled and chopped (about 4 cups)){3}
@Roma tomatoes (chopped (about 4 cups)){6}
@large red onion (chopped){1}
@feta cheese (cut into squares){8%ounces}
@olive oil{1/2%cup}
@red wine vinegar{1/4%cup}
@dried oregano{1/2%Tablespoon}
@minced garlic (3 cloves){1/2%Tablespoon}
@dijon mustard{1/2%teaspoon}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@pepper{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Combine cucumbers, tomatoes, onion, and feta in a large salad bowl.

Prepare dressing by combining all ingredients and shaking well.

Pour dressing over salad and stir to combine.
