-- Ingredients

@white whole wheat flour{3/4%cup}
@rolled oats{3/4%cup}
@white sugar{3%tablespoons}
@brown sugar{1/4%cup}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@canola oil (or unsalted butter, melted){1/3%cup}
@apples (peeled and diced){6%cups}
@white whole wheat flour{1%tablespoon}
@white sugar{3%tablespoons}

-- Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350 °F. Lightly grease a baking dish with butter or oil.

To make the topping: Place the flour, oats, sugars and salt in the mixing bowl and toss together. Add the oil (or butter) and toss again.

For the filling, fill the baking pan with the fruit and the remaining 1 tablespoon flour and 3 tablespoons sugar. Mix well and then cover with the topping.

Transfer to the oven and bake until the fruit is tender and the topping is golden, about 45 minutes. Serve right away or cover and refrigerate up to 2 days.
