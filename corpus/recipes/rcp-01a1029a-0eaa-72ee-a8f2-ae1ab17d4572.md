-- Ingredients

@canned pears, drained and chopped{1%cup}
@low fat yogurt{1%cup}
@frozen orange juice concentrate, thawed{6%ounces}
@small paper cups (or an ice cube tray){4}
@popsicle sticks (or cut paper straws){4}

-- Instructions

Cut canned pears into small pieces.

Mix fruit, yogurt, and orange juice.

Divide into 4 small paper cups (or 8 ice cubes) and place in popsicle sticks (or cut paper straws).

Freeze.

Enjoy as a frozen treat!
