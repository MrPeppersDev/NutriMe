>> servings: 4
>> source: http://www.playfulcooking.com/spice-and-condiment/hibiscus-syrup/
>> attribution: playfulcooking.com

-- Ingredients

@water{4%cups}
@dried hibiscus flowers{3/4%cup}
@inch ginger (finely grated){1}
@whole star anise{2}
@zest of 1 lemon{}
@sugar (add more if you like it sweeter){1%cup}
@salt{1/8%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

In a deep pan, mix all the ingredients and bring it to a boil.

Once it starts to boil, reduce the heat to medium and let it simmer for 10 minutes.

Turn off the heat, cover it with a lid and let it steep for 5 more minutes.

Strain it through a sieve and store the syrup in an airtight bottle.
