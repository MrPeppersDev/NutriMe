-- Ingredients

@canned peach halves, packed in juice{2}
@cottage cheese, low-fat (1 ounce){2%tablespoons}
@raisins{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Place one peach half, cut side up, in center of plate for nest.

Place 1 tablespoon of cottage cheese in center of nest.

Place raisins on cottage cheese to make eggs.

Enjoy your Eagle Nest.
