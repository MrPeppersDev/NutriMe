-- Ingredients

@90% lean ground beef (or other ground meat){1%pound}
@onion (chopped, or 1 Tbsp onion powder){1/2%cup}
@whole corn, low-sodium (15.2 ounce, or any low-sodium canned vegetable, drained){1%can}
@taco seasoning mix (dry, or dry enchilada sauce){4%tablespoons}
@brown rice, cooked{2%cups}
@cheese (grated){1/2%cup}
@mushrooms, olives or any favorite vegetable (1/2 cup sliced, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Cook meat and onion until onion juices are clear.

Drain juice and fat from cooked meat and onions.

Add corn, taco seasoning, and rice.

Simmer for 10 minutes.

Add grated cheese to top; cover and let set for 5 minutes.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
