-- Ingredients

@mini baguette or Italian bread, about 9" long (split lengthwise, or 2 split English muffins){1/4}
@pizza sauce{1/2%cup}
@part-skim mozzarella or cheddar cheese, shredded{1/2%cup}
@green pepper, chopped{1/4%cup}
@mushrooms, fresh or canned, sliced{1/4%cup}
@other vegetable toppings (as desired, optional){}
@Italian seasoning (optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Toast the bread or English muffin until slightly brown.

Top bread or muffin with pizza sauce, vegetables and low-fat cheese.

Sprinkle with Italian seasonings as desired.

Return bread to toaster oven (or regular oven preheated to 350 °F).

Heat until cheese melts.
