-- Ingredients

@mint leaves{1/4%cup}
@agave or honey{3/4%ounce}
@lime juice{3/4%ounce}
@water{1 1/2%cups}
@gin{2 1/2%ounces}
@matcha{1%teaspoon}
@ice

-- Instructions

In a cocktail shaker, muddle the mint with the agave and lime juice.

Add the remaining ingredients and shake until well chilled and the matcha is dissolved.

Strain through a fine-mesh strainer, dividing between 2 ice-filled rocks glasses, then serve.
