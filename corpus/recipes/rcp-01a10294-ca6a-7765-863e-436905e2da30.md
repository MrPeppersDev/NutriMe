-- Ingredients

@(15.5 ounces) black or red beans, low-sodium{1%can}
@(15.2 ounces) corn, low-sodium{1%can}
@fresh tomato salsa{1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Drain and rinse the beans. Drain the corn.

Combine beans, corn, and salsa in a medium-sized bowl. Mix.
