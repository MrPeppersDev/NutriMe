-- Ingredients

@eggs{4}
@sugar{1%cup}
@milk, 1%{4%cups}
@butter, melted{5%tablespoons}
@matzo meal{2%cups}
@salt{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine eggs and sugar; beat well.

Add milk and melted butter; mix well.

Stir in matzo meal and salt.

Pour into buttered 11x13 inch pan.

Bake at 350 °F for 1 hour (don't over bake).

Cut into slices. Can be served hot or cold.
