>> attribution: NHLBI Delicious Heart Healthy Eating

-- Ingredients

@water{3%C}
@cube (1 tsp) low-sodium vegetable bouillon{1}
@white potatoes, cut into 2-inch strips{2%C}
@carrots, sliced{2%C}
@summer squash, cut into 1-inch squares{4%C}
@summer squash, cut into 4 large chunks{1%C}
@(15 oz) sweet corn, rinsed and drained (or 2 ears fresh corn, 1 1/2 C){1%can}
@dried thyme{1%tsp}
@garlic, minced{2%cloves}
@scallion (green onion), chopped{1%stalk}
@small hot pepper, chopped{1/2}
@coarsely chopped onion{1%C}
@diced tomatoes{1%C}

-- Instructions

Put water and bouillon in a large pot, and bring to a boil.

Add potatoes and carrots, and simmer for 5 minutes.

Add remaining ingredients, except for tomatoes, and continue cooking for 15 minutes over medium heat.

Remove the four large chunks of squash and puree in a blender. Return pureed mixture to the pot, and let cook for 10 minutes more.

Add tomatoes and cook for another 5 minutes.

Remove from heat and let sit for 10 minutes to allow stew to thicken before serving.
