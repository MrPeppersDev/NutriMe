-- Ingredients

@water{5%cups}
@brown rice{2 1/2%cups}
@onion, chopped{1/4%cup}
@garlic clove, chopped{1}
@skim milk{1%cup}
@(10.75 ounces) condensed cream of mushroom soup, reduced-sodium{1%can}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1/4%teaspoon}
@low-fat cheddar cheese, grated{3/4%cup}
@chicken, cooked, shredded{2%cups}
@broccoli, chopped{2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 °F. In large saucepan, bring water to boil. Add rice, onion, and garlic. Cook for about 30 minutes or until rice is soft.

While rice is cooking combine milk, soup, salt, and pepper, mix well. When rice is done combine with milk mixture, chicken, and broccoli. Mix well.

Grease 9x13 pan and pour mixture into pan. Bake in preheated oven for 18 minutes. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake for another 6 minutes or until cheese is melted.

Serve immediately.
