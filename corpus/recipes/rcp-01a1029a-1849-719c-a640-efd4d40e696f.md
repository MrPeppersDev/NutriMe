>> title: Fruit and Peanut Butter Dip
>> servings: 6
>> source: https://www.myplate.gov/recipes/fruit-and-peanut-butter-dip
>> attribution: USDA MyPlate Kitchen
>> description: Use yogurt, peanut butter, and vanilla to create a dip for your favorite fruits.
>> calories_per_serving: 180.50
>> original_contributor: Eating Smart, Being Active Colorado State University and University of California at Davis
>> notes: Can use six cups of any washed fruit. Be creative! Spread dip on a tortilla, add banana, and roll up. Learn more about: Apples Bananas Pears Grapes Strawberries Cantaloupe Honeydew

-- Ingredients

@apple slices{1%cup}
@banana slices{1%cup}
@pear slices{1%cup}
@grapes{1%cup}
@strawberries{1%cup}
@melon slices{1%cup}
@yogurt, non-fat plain{1/2%cup}
@vanilla{1/2%teaspoon}
@peanut butter{1/3%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Prepare fruit by washing and slicing if necessary.

Arrange fruit on a plate around a small bowl.

To make dip, combine yogurt, vanilla, and peanut butter in a small bowl.

Mix well.

Chill dip in refrigerator until ready to serve.
