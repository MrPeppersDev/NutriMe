-- Ingredients

@onion, chopped{1%cup}
@celery, chopped{3/4%cup}
@garlic, chopped{2%teaspoons}
@beef broth, reduced sodium, fat removed{1 1/2%cups}
@(15 ounces each) black beans, low-sodium, drained and rinsed{2%cans}
@thick and chunky salsa{1/2%cup}
@cumin{1 1/2%teaspoons}
@onion powder{1/2%teaspoon}
@dried oregano{1/4%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients in a medium or large saucepan.

Cover and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes or until vegetables are tender.
