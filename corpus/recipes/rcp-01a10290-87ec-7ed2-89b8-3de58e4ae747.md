-- Ingredients

@broccoli, cooked{4%cups}
@whole wheat pasta, cooked{4%cups}
@1% milk, divided (1/8 cup or 2 tablespoons is used later in the recipe){2 1/8%cups}
@dried basil{1%teaspoon}
@garlic powder{1/2%teaspoon}
@Parmesan cheese, reduced fat{1%cup}
@cornstarch{2%tablespoons}
@black pepper (to taste, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Placed cooked broccoli and pasta in a bowl.

Heat milk over medium heat and then add basil and garlic powder. When hot, add Parmesan cheese.

Mix cornstarch with 2 or 3 tablespoons of milk and add to hot mixture. Heat until thickened.

Pour mixture over pasta and broccoli. Serve.
