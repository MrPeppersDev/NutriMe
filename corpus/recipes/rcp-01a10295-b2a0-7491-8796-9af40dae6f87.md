-- Ingredients

@(15.5 ounces) low-sodium kidney beans{1%can}
@(15 ounces) low-sodium black beans, drained{1%can}
@(15 ounces) low-sodium corn, drained{1%can}
@(14.5 ounces) low-sodium crushed tomatoes{1%can}
@(4 ounces) chopped green chilies{1%can}
@finely chopped onion{1/4%cup}
@oil{1%tablespoon}
@limes, juiced (3 limes, optional){}
@salt (to taste, optional){}
@black pepper (to taste, optional){}
@hot sauce (to taste, optional){}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix kidney beans, black beans, corn, tomatoes, chilies, and onion in a large bowl.

Add oil and lime juice (if using), toss gently to combine. Taste. Add small amount of salt and pepper if desired.

Serve by itself, with raw vegetables and/or corn chips.
