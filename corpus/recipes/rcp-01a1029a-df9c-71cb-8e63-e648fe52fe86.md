-- Ingredients

@medium bananas{2}
@raisins (4 raisins){1/3%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Peel banana and use butter knife to cut banana in half lengthwise.

Place 2 banana halves on small plate end to end, in an "s" shape.

Add raisins to the top of banana as eyes.

Enjoy your Fruit Snake.
