-- Ingredients

@honeydew melon (scooped into balls){1}
@blackberries{1%cup}
@watercress{1%cup}
@shallot (thinly sliced){1}
@olive oil{3%tablespoons}
@juice of one lemon{}
@honey{1%tablespoon}
@sea salt{1%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Combine the honeydew, blackberries, watercress, and shallots in a bowl.

Combine the olive oil, lemon juice, honey, and salt in a small bowl and whisk briskly to combine.

Dress the salad and serve.
