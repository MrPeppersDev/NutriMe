-- Ingredients

@garlic powder{1 1/2%teaspoons}
@onion powder{1%tablespoon}
@oregano (ground or leaves){2%tablespoons}
@parsley (dried){1%tablespoon}
@sugar{1%tablespoon}
@salt (or substitute){2%tablespoons}
@black pepper{1%teaspoon}
@basil (dried){1%teaspoon}
@thyme (dried, ground){1/4%teaspoon}
@celery ( dried leaves or flakes){1/2%teaspoon}

-- Instructions

Mix all ingredients together and store in an air-tight container

Mix 2 tbsp of this mix with 1/4 cup vinegar, two tbs water, and 1/2 to 2/3 cup olive oil or canola oil.

Shake before using.
