-- Ingredients

@whole grain bread{4%slices}
@cooked chicken (diced){1/2%cup}
@canned peach slices (drained and diced){1/2%cup}
@celery stalk{1}
@apples (Fuji, Gala, or Braeburn){1/2%cup}
@small onion{1}
@mayonnaise (nonfat){1 1/2%tablespoons}
@chopped walnut{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Mix together the chicken, apples, peaches, celery, onion, walnuts, and mayonnaise in a small bowl.

Spoon mixture onto 2 slices of the bread.
