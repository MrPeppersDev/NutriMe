-- Ingredients

@green cabbage{1/4%head}
@carrots{2}
@raisins{1/4%cup}
@peanuts{1/4%cup}
@low-fat vanilla yogurt{1/2%cup}
@orange juice (1 tablespoon){1/16%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Slice and cut cabbage into thin strips. Place pieces in large mixing bowl.

Peel and grate carrots. Add to mixing bowl.

Add raisins and peanuts to mixing bowl.

Mix all ingredients together.

Combine yogurt and orange juice in small bowl.

Add yogurt mixture to cabbage mixture, stirring to combine ingredients thoroughly.
