-- Ingredients

@zucchini chunks{3%C}
@small yellow onion, sliced{1}
@medium tomato, diced{1}
@chopped mushroom{1/2%C}
@dried basil{1/2%tsp}
@(2 oz) low-fat cheddar cheese, shredded{1/2%C}

-- Instructions

Place all ingredients except the cheese in a microwave-safe 2-quart casserole dish.

Microwave on high for 7 to 10 minutes, or until vegetables are fork tender.

Add the cheese, and microwave on high for 30 seconds.

Let stand for 2 to 3 minutes before serving.
