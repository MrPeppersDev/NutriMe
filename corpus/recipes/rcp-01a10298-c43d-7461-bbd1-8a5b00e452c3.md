-- Ingredients

@medium cucumbers, diced into 1/4 inch pieces{2}
@medium red bell peppers, seeded and diced into 1/4 inch pieces{3}
@medium green peppers, seeded and diced into 1/4 inch pieces{3}
@medium celery stalks, diced into 1/4 inch pieces{4}
@medium tomatoes, diced into 1/4 inch pieces{2}
@medium onion, diced into 1/4 inch pieces{1}
@low-sodium tomato juice{2%cups}
@garlic cloves, minced{3}
@ground cumin{1%tablespoon}
@fresh cilantro, chopped{1%cup}
@salt and pepper (to taste, optional){}
@lemons, juiced{2}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Combine all ingredients except salt, pepper, and lemons in a bowl.

Remove 2 cups of the mixture and reserve.

Using a blender or food processor, puree the remaining mixture in the bowl.

Add 2 cups of reserved mixture to the pureed mixture.

Season with salt, pepper (optional) and the juice from the lemons.

Cover mixture and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.

Serve cold, garnished with chopped cilantro.
