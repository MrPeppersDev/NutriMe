-- Ingredients

@water{2%cups}
@broccoli florets{1%cup}
@cauliflower florets{1%cup}
@zucchini, small (sliced){2}
@onion, small (diced){1}
@celery stalks (chopped){3}
@tomato sauce, low-sodium (8 ounces){1%can}
@basil{2%teaspoons}
@salt (1 teaspoon, optional){}
@package of pasta, any shape (cooked){1%pound}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Put 1 cup of hot water in a saucepan.

Add vegetables and cook for 5 minutes.

Add tomato sauce, remaining cup of water, basil and salt.

Simmer until heated thoroughly.

Serve with cooked pasta.

Refrigerate leftovers.
