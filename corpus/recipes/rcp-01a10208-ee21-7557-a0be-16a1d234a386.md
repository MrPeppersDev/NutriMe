-- Ingredients

@Cucumber{1%sliced}
@Tomatoes{3%Cups}
@Red Onions{1/4}
@Green Pepper{1%chopped}
@Salt{1%tsp}
@Olive Oil{1/4%cup}
@Feta{1/3%cup}
@Parsley{2%tbs chopped}

-- Instructions

Combine vegetables in a bowl and sprinkle salt on top. Let it stand for 5 minutes. Add the oil. Top with feta and parsley. Serve.
