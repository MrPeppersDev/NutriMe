-- Ingredients

@Large Oranges{2}
@Bottle Merlot Wine{1}
@Brandy{1/3%Cup}
@Maple Syrup{2%Tablespoons}
@Cinnamon Sticks{2}
@Star Anise{4}
@Whole Cloves{4}
@Fresh Cranberries{1/2%Cup}

-- Instructions

Start by slicing one orange in half, and the second orange into even slices. Set aside your oranges once they’re cut.

Next, heat a large pot on the stove over low-medium heat.

Add in your orange slices, then squeeze the juice from the orange halves into the pot.

Then, pour in your merlot, followed by the brandy, maple syrup, and spices, stirring to fully incorporate.

Turn the heat up to medium to warm the mixture; just be sure to watch it carefully, so it doesn’t boil to bubble up (we just want it to steam). This should take about 4-5 minutes at most.

Next, reduce the heat to low and taste; if it’s not sweet enough for your liking, you can add in a dash more maple syrup. If you want it spicier, let it sit for up to another 5 minutes so the spices infuse.

Serve in mugs, garnish, and enjoy!
