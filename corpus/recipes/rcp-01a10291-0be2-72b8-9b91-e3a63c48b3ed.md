-- Ingredients

@vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice){1%tablespoon}
@medium onion, chopped{1}
@chicken broth, low-sodium{2%cups}
@boneless chicken or turkey, cooked, and diced{2%cups}
@tomatoes, low sodium canned or cooked{2%cups}
@lima beans, cooked (or canned, low-sodium){2%cups}
@whole kernel corn, canned, low-sodium (or cooked){2%cups}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Heat oil in a large pan. Add onion and cook in oil until tender.

Add all remaining ingredients. Bring to a simmer for 30 minutes at medium-low.

Makes 8 servings of about 1 cup each.
