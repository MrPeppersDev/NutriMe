-- Ingredients

@melon, honeydew, cantaloupe, or watermelon, fresh (seeded and chopped, use one kind or a combination){2%cups}
@cucumber (peeled, seeded and chopped){1%cup}
@onion, red or white (chopped){1/4%cup}
@cilantro or mint, fresh (2 tablespoon, chopped, optional){}
@jalapeño (seeded and finely chopped, or hot sauce to taste){1/2}
@lime juice or lemon juice{1/4%cup}
@sugar, white or brown{1%tablespoon}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

In a medium size bowl, stir together all ingredients.

Taste and season with more lemon or lime juice, sugar if needed.

Cover and chill for at least 30 minutes. Serve with grilled or broiled fish or chicken.
