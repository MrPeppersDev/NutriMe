-- Ingredients

@frozen mixed vegetables, thawed{1 2/3%cups}
@cooked chicken, diced{1%cup}
@(10.75 ounces) condensed cream of chicken soup, reduced-sodium{1%can}
@baking mix, reduced-fat{1%cup}
@milk, fat-free{1/2%cup}
@egg{1}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Pre-heat oven to 400 °F.

Mix vegetables, chicken, and soup in ungreased, 9-inch pie plate

Stir baking mix, milk, and egg in a mixing bowl with fork until blended. Pour over vegetables and chicken in pie plate.

Bake 30 minutes or until golden brown.

Let cool for 5 minutes and serve.
