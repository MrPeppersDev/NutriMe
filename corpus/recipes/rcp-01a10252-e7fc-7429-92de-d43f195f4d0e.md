-- Ingredients

@chicken thighs{6-8}
@white pepper{1/2%teaspoon}
@black pepper{1%teaspoon}
@garlic powder{1/2%teaspoon}
@onion powder{1/2%teaspoon}
@olive oil{2%tablespoons}
@heavy cream{12%ounces}
@all-purpose flour{2%tablespoons}
@butter (melted){1/4%cup}
@olive oil{1%teaspoon}
@garlic (minced){5%cloves}
@dried chives{1%tablespoon}
@fresh parsley{1/2%tablespoon}
@freshly grated parmesan cheese{1/2%cup}
@black pepper{1%teaspoon}
@salt{1/2%teaspoon}
@fresh asparagus (cut the ends off and cut them into 3 inch strips){1%pound}

-- Instructions

In a large non stick skillet , heat the oil on medium

Take your chicken thighs and season them with all the ingredients that are not 'sauce' ingredients

Place them one by one into the hot skillet and cook for about 15 minutes, or until browned on both sides, flipping as needed, remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside while you make the sauce.

In a large mixing bowl, combine all sauce ingredients EXCEPT GARLIC & 1 teaspoon olive oil & mix until well combined.

In the same skillet you used for the chicken, dd in the 1 teaspoon olive oil and the minced garlic and cook about 1-2 minutes on medium, then pour the sauce mixture in with the garlic & bring to a boil for about 3 minutes or so, mixing constantly.

reduce heat and add in the asparagus and the chicken, simmer until asparagus is tender, about 5-7 min on LOW covered.

ENJOY!
