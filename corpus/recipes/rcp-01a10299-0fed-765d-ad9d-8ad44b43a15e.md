-- Ingredients

@cereal with fruit (flakes with raisins type){1%cup}
@bran cereal (chex type){1%cup}
@cereal ("O" type){1%cup}
@raisins{1/4%cup}
@peanuts{1/4%cup}
@shredded coconut{1/4%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix cereals together in a large bowl.

Add raisins, peanuts, and coconut; mix well.

Eat it dry or with milk.
