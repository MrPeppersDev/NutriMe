-- Ingredients

@Rice Flour{1/2%cup}
@Wheat Flour{1/2%cup}
@Coconut Milk{1/2%cup}
@Palm Sugar{3/4%cup}
@Eggs{2}
@Salt{1/2%tsp}

-- Instructions

In a bowl, mix the flour, coconut milk, palm sugar and eggs and salt. Brush a waffle iron with oil pour in the batter and cook over an open fire until light brown and crispy.
