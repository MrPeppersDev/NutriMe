-- Ingredients

@rapid rise yeast{1%teaspoon}
@lukewarm water{1/2%cup}
@salt{1/4%teaspoon}
@oil{1%teaspoon}
@bread flour (approximately){1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Mix yeast, water, salt, and oil. Add about 1 cup flour to make a stiff dough. The amount of flour may vary slightly.

Knead the dough until it is very elastic (will stretch and not make a hole).

Use a rolling pin or clean bottle to obtain even thickness.

Lay it on a greased baking sheet to rise while you prepare toppings for pizza.

Bake at 400 °F with desired toppings until crust is browned and top is bubbly.
