-- Ingredients

@margarine or butter{1/2%cup}
@brown sugar (firmly packed){3/4%cup}
@vanilla{1%teaspoon}
@baking soda{1/2%teaspoon}
@quick oats (uncooked){2%cups}
@raisins{1%cup}

-- Instructions

Wash hands with soap and water.

Preheat oven to 350 °F and grease a 9x13 inch baking pan.

In large skillet, melt butter and brown sugar.

Remove from heat and stir in remaining ingredients.

Spread into pan and bake for 15 to 20 minutes. Cool; cut into squares.
